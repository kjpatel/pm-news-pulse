# Claude Fable 5.2 review: fast, creative, and finally not annoying

**Source**: [Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/claude-fable-52-review-fast-creative)
**Author**: Lenny Rachitsky (featuring Claire Vo) | **Date**: Nov 09, 2026

---

## Summary

Claire Vo tests Anthropic's new Fable 5.2 model through hands-on projects, including video generation, spatial reasoning, real engineering, and game building, and finds it strong at creative and technical work but still weak at web design. She argues it should be routed to specific tasks alongside Opus 5.5 rather than used as a universal default.

## Key Takeaways

- **Route creative and multimodal work to Fable 5.2:** Its strengths in animated video generation from a simple brief (like a single tweet and brand book) make it a good first choice for marketing assets that shouldn't look like slide decks.
- **Test spatial reasoning with ambiguous inputs:** Try a task like assembling flat-pack furniture from blurry photos with no manual to see how a model handles geometry, since many models failed this test.
- **Use it for substantive engineering refactors:** Fable 5.2 handled a real re-architecture of a product intelligence pipeline, so it's worth piloting on complex codebase changes you'd otherwise hand to a senior engineer.
- **Keep humans in charge of web design:** Even with strong results elsewhere, don't let the model design a website autonomously; review its output closely and direct the design decisions yourself.
- **Build a routing habit between models:** Decide per task whether Fable 5.2 or Opus 5.5 is the better fit, and track which one wins on your common workloads so the choice becomes deliberate rather than default.
