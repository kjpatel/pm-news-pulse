# How to make people care about your startup

**Source**: [Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-make-people-care-about-your)
**Author**: Lenny Rachitsky (featuring Kristen Lowe) | **Date**: Aug 11, 2026

---

## Summary

Founder-led communication rooted in your authentic 'why' is one of the highest-leverage, lowest-cost tools to build startup awareness and loyalty. By identifying your founder archetype—Problem, Insight, or Vision—you can craft a strategic messaging approach that evokes the right emotional connection with your audience.

## Key Takeaways

- **Identify your founder archetype** (Problem, Insight, or Vision) to determine whether your audience should identify with you, trust you, or follow you—this guides your entire comms strategy.
- **Lead with your personal origin story** using specific, vivid scene-based details rather than generic problem statements; this creates emotional resonance and helps customers feel genuinely understood.
- **Commit to any sustainable cadence over perfection**; the best founder communications strategy is the one you can consistently execute, not the theoretically optimal one.
- **Use high-repetition vignette-style storytelling** across channels to reinforce your core narrative and make it memorable for your audience.
- **Align your voice, messaging pillars, and success metrics** to your archetype rather than chasing vanity metrics like follower counts—focus on building genuine affiliation and loyalty.
