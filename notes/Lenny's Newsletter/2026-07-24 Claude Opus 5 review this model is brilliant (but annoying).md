# Claude Opus 5 review: this model is brilliant (but annoying)

**Source**: [Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/claude-opus-5-review-this-model-is)
**Author**: Lenny Rachitsky | **Date**: Jul 24, 2026

---

## Summary

Lenny Rachitsky provides a hands-on review of Claude Opus 5, testing it against competing models using his proprietary How I AI benchmark across 7 models and 6 tasks, finding that while the model shows strong intelligence, its verbose personality and refusal behaviors make it less practical than expected for real-world use cases.

## Key Takeaways

- **Run blind benchmarks** to avoid bias when comparing AI models—use standardized evaluation criteria across multiple tasks and models scored without knowing which produced which output.
- **Test personality fit, not just intelligence** when choosing production models, as Opus 5's neurotic refusal patterns (like avoiding merge conflict resolution) can hinder workflows despite strong technical capabilities.
- **Watch for the intelligence overhang** where raw benchmark improvements no longer translate to better user experiences; focus on practical variables like verbosity, personality traits, and actual use case performance instead.
