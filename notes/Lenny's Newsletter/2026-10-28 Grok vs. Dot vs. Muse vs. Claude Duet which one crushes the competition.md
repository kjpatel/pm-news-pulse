# Grok vs. Dot vs. Muse vs. Claude Duet: which one crushes the competition?

**Source**: [Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/grok-vs-dot-vs-muse-vs-claude-duet)
**Author**: Lenny Rachitsky (featuring Claire Vo) | **Date**: Oct 28, 2026

---

## Summary

Claire Vo ran the first How I AI personal-agent benchmark, testing Grok Bot, Dot, Meta Muse, and Claude Duet across nine real tasks, from work chores like Slack replies and bug fixes to family and personal errands. She uses the results to argue for which agent to trust for work versus life.

## Key Takeaways

- **Benchmark agents on real, end-to-end tasks.** Test each personal agent on concrete jobs from your own week, such as drafting a reply, triaging an inbox, or fixing a bug, rather than relying on demos or feature lists.
- **Score results consistently and record them.** Use a fixed rubric and have a single evaluator, such as Codex in this case, score every output so comparisons across agents stay fair.
- **Separate work trust from life trust.** Decide which agent you would let act on your behalf for professional tasks like sending messages or touching production systems, and which you would use only for personal errands.
- **Use computer use only where APIs fall short.** Some agents were driven through computer use to reach apps they cannot access directly, so note where this adds friction or risk before handing it routine work.
- **Keep personal-agent evaluations lightweight and repeatable.** Pick a small set of recurring tasks, such as a Slack reply, a short video cut, or a launch asset, and rerun them whenever a new agent or model ships.
