# How this founder built a better Duolingo with Codex | William Imoh

**Source**: [Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-this-founder-built-a-better-duolingo)
**Author**: Lenny Rachitsky (featuring William Imoh) | **Date**: Nov 09, 2026

---

## Summary

William Imoh, founder of Hackmamba, explains how he used Codex and GPT-Live to build Mural, a native iOS and Android voice app for learning Norwegian, after 1,000 days of Duolingo left him unable to hold a conversation. He also describes running his company with a team of AI agents organized as a Lord of the Rings-themed fellowship.

## Key Takeaways

- **Write a precise design prompt before building.** Imoh credits a detailed design language and clear articulation of the look he wanted with making Mural look nothing like other language apps.
- **Measure voice latency as a core product constraint.** For a voice tutor, response latency determines whether the conversation feels real, so track it alongside per-session model costs such as GPT-Live-1 pricing.
- **Build outside your existing skill set with AI coding tools.** Imoh shipped a native Swift app despite never having written Swift, so use tools like Codex to close skill gaps instead of waiting to learn the language first.
- **Give each agent a clearly defined role and scoped access.** Assign every solo-founder function to a specific agent persona, and deliberately withhold access to certain systems rather than granting access to everything.
- **Keep ownership of the tasks that models still handle poorly.** Identify the work you must still do yourself, and guard against growing dependency on agents rather than focusing only on fear of replacement.
