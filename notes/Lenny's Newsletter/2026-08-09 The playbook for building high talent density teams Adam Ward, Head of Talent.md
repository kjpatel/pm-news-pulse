# The playbook for building high talent density teams | Adam Ward, Head of Talent at Cursor

## Summary

Adam Ward, Head of Talent at Cursor, shares a three-step playbook for building high-talent-density teams by replacing traditional recruiting funnels with strategic scoping, targeted mapping, and relentless pursuit of top talent.

## Key Takeaways

- **Replace the 'funnel of doom'** with a three-step process: scoping (clearly defining what you need), mapping (identifying and understanding target candidates), and relentless pursuit (persistent, creative outreach) to avoid mediocre hires from traditional recruiting channels.
- **Master the talent market split** by recognizing the current 'tale of two cities'—understanding which talent segments are abundant versus scarce, and focusing your recruiting energy on the truly competitive markets where differentiation matters.
- **Ask better sourcing questions** by avoiding generic inquiries and instead using targeted, creative approaches that demonstrate deep understanding of candidates' goals, values, and career trajectories to attract top talent.
- **Consider forward-deployed engineers** as a recruiting strategy—engineers who embed directly with customers or stakeholders bring unique perspectives and can become your best talent advocates and recruiters.
- **Hire your first recruiter strategically** by avoiding the common founder mistake of delegating recruiting too early; instead, master the recruiting process yourself first so you can properly guide and evaluate your recruiting team.
