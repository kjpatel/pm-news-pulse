## Summary

OpenAI's Nick Baumann demonstrates advanced ChatGPT Codex workflows that automate parallel tasks through voice, browser control, and AI-powered tools like thread-forking and heartbeats, while ChatGPT Sites enables rapid deployment of functional applications without traditional development setup.

## Key Takeaways

- **Use voice as your primary interface** for giving AI context—the 'yapper's API' approach often produces better results than carefully typed prompts because people naturally provide more contextual information when speaking freely.
- **Leverage thread-forking for complex tasks**—Codex can autonomously branch conversations, open new threads, and pull context from previous discussions, eliminating the need to manually organize everything upfront.
- **Deploy internal tools at production speed**—ChatGPT Sites includes SQL databases, file storage, and access controls, enabling you to build and publish functional apps in minutes rather than days of CMS configuration.
- **Automate repetitive content workflows end-to-end**—Apply Codex to transcribe, analyze, select, and assemble content (like video editing) overnight, freeing your team from manual production work.
- **Prioritize latency over marginal model improvements**—Once AI has the right tools, speed matters more than slightly better reasoning; design for immediate responsiveness to enable real-time integration into workflows.
