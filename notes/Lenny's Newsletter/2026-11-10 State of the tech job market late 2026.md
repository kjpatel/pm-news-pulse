# State of the tech job market: late 2026

**Source**: [Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/state-of-the-tech-job-market-late)
**Author**: Lenny Rachitsky | **Date**: Nov 10, 2026

---

## Summary

Lenny's late-2026 tech job market report argues that PMs are the biggest winners in the AI era, with open PM roles at a near four-year high, while engineering demand has plateaued and design and user research roles are at record lows. It also finds that AI skills are now expected across tech roles and that hiring is concentrating in the Bay Area and outside traditional software.

## Key Takeaways

- **Position yourself for PM roles now, since demand is outpacing supply:** Open PM roles are up 75% from the March 2023 low, but PM hiring is flat, so landing a strong PM job is getting harder and candidates who stand out have an edge.
- **Build visible AI fluency into your resume and portfolio:** "LLM" is now the most-mentioned skill in PM job descriptions, with Claude, Cursor, OpenAI, and MCP also in the top eight, so list and demonstrate concrete AI tool use.
- **Consider forward deployed engineering (FDE) or security engineering if you're an engineer:** FDE openings are up 4.6x in a year, and security engineering is among the fastest-growing specialties, while mobile and front-end demand has nearly halved.
- **Strengthen your design and research skills with AI-driven product work:** Product design roles are at a record low with about 1,900 open globally, so pair design craft with AI tooling and show how it improves differentiation in a crowded, AI-generated market.
- **Target Bay Area and non-software employers when searching:** Tech jobs are concentrating in the Bay Area, remote roles are at a record low, and growth is strongest outside software, so widen your search beyond remote software companies.
