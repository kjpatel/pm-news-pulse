## Key Takeaways

- **Anchor your counteroffer on total compensation.** When an offer comes in low, respond with a specific, data-backed counter that covers base, equity, and bonus rather than rejecting it outright.
- **Make designers' input visible early in product decisions.** Invite designers into problem-definition and prioritization conversations, not just solution review, so their perspective shapes the work from the start.
- **Start with official MCP server documentation and a minimal example.** Build a small working server first, then add tools and resources incrementally, so you can validate the integration before scaling it.
- **Use peer communities to pressure-test career and team decisions.** Bring specific situations to trusted peers and compare how others handled similar offers, roles, or stakeholder conflicts.
