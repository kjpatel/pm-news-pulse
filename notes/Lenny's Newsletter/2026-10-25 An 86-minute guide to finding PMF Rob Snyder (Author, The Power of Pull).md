# An 86-minute guide to finding PMF | Rob Snyder (Author, The Power of Pull)

**Source**: [Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/an-86-minute-guide-to-finding-pmf)
**Author**: Lenny Rachitsky (featuring Rob Snyder) | **Date**: Oct 25, 2026

---

## Summary

Rob Snyder walks through his PULL framework (Project, Unavoidable, List of options, Limitations) for finding product-market fit, from generating an idea to closing the first handful of deals. He argues that real demand, not founder conviction, should drive every step.

## Key Takeaways

- **Map your idea against the PULL framework:** Identify the Project, test whether the problem is Unavoidable for customers, list the alternatives they already use, and state the Limitations of your approach before investing heavily.
- **Skip design partners as a shortcut to validation:** Snyder advises against them, so look for buyers who would pay for the solution rather than customers who are only being polite about feedback.
- **Run cold outreach and sales calls early:** Reach out directly to prospects and run your first sales conversations yourself to learn what actually moves someone to buy.
- **Set a plan for when nothing is working:** Snyder's "goblin mode" idea is to change tactics deliberately when traction stalls, rather than continuing the same approach or giving up.
- **Apply PULL inside larger companies:** Use the same demand-first test for internal projects by checking whether the need is unavoidable for the people you serve, not just whether leadership wants it.
