## Summary

This Community Wisdom edition curates the most valuable discussions from Lenny's subscriber Slack community, covering practical topics like authentic outreach strategies, design partnership approaches, competitive moats in an accessible AI world, and techniques for making product visions resonate with teams.

## Key Takeaways

- **Craft authentic outreach** by focusing on genuine value exchange rather than generic pitches—community members share how personalization and specificity significantly improve response rates.
- **Find design partners strategically** by identifying collaborators who share your vision and constraints, rather than treating design as a purely transactional service.
- **Reframe moats in the AI era** since traditional defensibility assumptions have shifted; focus on distribution, speed of execution, and brand trust as sustainable advantages when tools are commoditized.
- **Make product vision sticky** through consistent reinforcement, clear communication of 'why,' and alignment of daily work with long-term goals to prevent strategy drift.
- **Leverage peer wisdom systematically** by participating in community conversations to learn from others' experiments and mistakes before implementing in your own context.
