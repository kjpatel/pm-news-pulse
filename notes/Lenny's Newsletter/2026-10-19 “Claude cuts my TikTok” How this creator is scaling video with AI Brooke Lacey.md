## Summary

Brooke Lacey shows how she uses Claude Code, FFmpeg, and Whisper to edit raw phone videos into TikToks in about 15 seconds, turning audience comments into scripts. The result is a weekly short-form operation she runs without an editor, built on a system she taught Claude to follow.

## Key Takeaways

- **Build a scripted edit pipeline with Claude Code:** Combine FFmpeg for cutting and Whisper for transcription so Claude can edit a raw phone video in seconds, then review the output rather than editing manually.
- **Teach Claude your editing style explicitly:** Document your jump-cut cadence, hook patterns, and what counts as funny so the agent reproduces your style consistently across videos.
- **Mine your comments for content ideas:** Use recurring questions and reactions in your TikTok comments as the basis for new scripts, so each video answers a demand your audience has already expressed.
- **Use status files and lanes to keep the agent on track:** When Claude drifts or disappoints, keep state in status files and separate task lanes, then prompt it back to the defined process instead of starting over.
- **Optimize for consistency over frequency:** Maintain a sustainable weekly posting cadence with an automated workflow, since a repeatable system matters more than posting every day with no editor support.
