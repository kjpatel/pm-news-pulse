## Key Takeaways

- **Audit where you gate your AI agents:** Map every decision, approval, and context handoff that currently routes through you, and delegate or document the ones that don't need your judgment.
- **Apply the PULL framework to your product decisions:** Use it as a structured checklist to evaluate whether a product bet is grounded in real user pull rather than internal push.
- **Separate product growth from growth marketing:** Assign product-led levers such as activation, onboarding, and retention to the product team, and reserve acquisition campaigns for marketing, so each has clear ownership and metrics.
- **Raise feedback response rates with a shorter, specific ask:** Send fewer, more targeted requests, explain how the feedback will be used, and close the loop with respondents to build trust for future replies.
- **Pull the community's playbooks into your own workflow:** Scan the linked Slack threads for the tactics peers have tested, and adapt one or two to your team this week rather than trying to implement everything.
