## Summary

This week's Community Wisdom edition gathers member Slack discussions on practical product and career questions, including writing PRDs for agents, tracking competitor messaging, whether software moats still exist, and exercising startup options when leaving a company. The provided text contains only the introduction, so the takeaways below are drawn from the topic titles.

## Key Takeaways

- **Rethink the PRD for agents:** Define an agent's expected behavior with explicit goals, constraints, tools, and success criteria, since agents need more precise specifications than human-facing features.
- **Track competitor messaging systematically:** Save competitors' homepages, pricing pages, and launch posts on a regular schedule so you can spot shifts in positioning before they show up in sales calls.
- **Test whether your moat is real:** Ask whether a competitor with the same model access and distribution could replicate your product in a few months, and invest in advantages like proprietary data, workflow lock-in, or distribution.
- **Check your startup options before you resign:** Confirm your exercise window, strike price, tax implications, and whether exercising early makes sense given your financial position and the company's prospects.
