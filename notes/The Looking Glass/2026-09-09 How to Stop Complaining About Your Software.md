# How to Stop Complaining About Your Software

**Source**: [The Looking Glass](https://lg.substack.com/p/i-never-want-to-use-third-party-software)
**Author**: Julie Zhuo | **Date**: Sep 09, 2026

---

## Summary

The article argues that software is entering an era of digital hyperpersonalization, where tools adapt to individual users rather than forcing everyone into one fixed interface. The provided text is only the opening line, so this summary is inferred from the title and that line.

## Key Takeaways

- **List the specific frustrations you have with your current tools** so you can judge which personalized features would actually fix them.
- **Test whether your software lets you customize views, workflows, or settings** before accepting its default experience as the only option.
- **Give feedback to vendors in concrete terms** (what you wanted to do, where it got in the way) since personalization depends on signals from users.
- **Audit the tools your team uses most** and pick one workflow to tailor first, rather than trying to reconfigure everything at once.
- **Watch for privacy tradeoffs** when personalization requires tools to learn from your data, and review what each product collects.
