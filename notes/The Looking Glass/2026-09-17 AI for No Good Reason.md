# AI for No Good Reason

**Source**: [The Looking Glass](https://lg.substack.com/p/a-bird-wall-a-talking-rotary-phone)
**Author**: Julie Zhuo | **Date**: Sep 17, 2026

---

## Summary

The article argues that AI's value extends beyond work productivity, showcasing creative ways people use it purely for leisure and enjoyment. Its subtitle frames the piece as a survey of the most inventive personal uses of AI.

## Key Takeaways

- **Experiment with play first:** Spend a fixed 20 minutes a week trying AI on a hobby, game, or creative whim with no productivity goal, and note what surprises you.
- **Treat AI as a creative partner:** Try prompting AI to generate a story, song, or game concept you would never have written alone, then remix the output by hand.
- **Share your unusual uses:** Post one playful AI experiment with your friends or team to see which ideas spread and which ones spark new ones.
- **Judge AI by delight, not just output:** When evaluating a tool, add a question about whether it makes a leisure activity more fun, not only whether it saves time.
