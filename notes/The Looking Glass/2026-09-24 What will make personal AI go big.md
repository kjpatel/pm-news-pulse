# What will make personal AI go big?

**Source**: [The Looking Glass](https://lg.substack.com/p/what-will-make-personal-ai-go-big)
**Author**: Julie Zhuo | **Date**: Sep 24, 2026

---

## Summary

The article asks what will make personal AI go mainstream, using Muse's appeal as the case study. It argues that four design principles explain why Muse resonates with users. The full body text is not included, so this summary is based on the title and subtitle.

## Key Takeaways

- **Design for everyday personal use:** Build personal AI around the routine tasks people actually want help with, rather than around impressive demos.
- **Make the experience feel personal from the start:** Give users a sense that the assistant understands their context and preferences, since personalization is central to Muse's appeal.
- **Prioritize consumer-grade UX:** Make setup and interaction simple enough that non-technical users can adopt the product without learning new workflows.
- **Study Muse's four principles before copying them:** Document which of the article's four design principles apply to your product and test each one with real users.
- **Judge personal AI by adoption, not capability:** Track whether users return daily or weekly, since mass adoption depends on habit formation more than raw model performance.
