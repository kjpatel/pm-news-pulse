# Inside Clay’s GTM machine

**Source**: [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/inside-clay-s-gtm-machine)
**Author**: Kyle Poyar (featuring Osman Sheikhnureldin) | **Date**: Nov 08, 2026

---

## Summary

Clay's head of GTM engineering walks through 17 automated GTM workflows, showing how the company uses automation for account scoring, routing, and rep prep while keeping human relationships central. The article argues that the strongest GTM teams excel at both automation and relationships rather than sitting in the middle.

## Key Takeaways

- **Build a multi-input fit score for every account.** Enrich accounts with firmographic and custom AI research fields, then score them 0-100 on company size, business model, GTM motion, sales headcount, tech stack, funding stage, and revenue trajectory, and write the result to your CRM.
- **Route accounts by fit score and contact seniority.** Send qualified accounts with decision makers straight to an AE, qualified non-decision-makers to an SDR, and unqualified accounts to a self-serve path.
- **Combine fit, predicted contract value, intent, and engagement into one propensity score.** Surface the top 5 accounts per rep each week so reps know exactly where to spend their time.
- **Use human-in-the-loop for automated outreach.** Generate outreach drafts automatically for triggers like new executive hires or job changes, but have reps edit and approve them before sending.
- **Automate the prep work around every meeting.** Send pre-demo emails three days ahead tailored to the lead form submission, and generate pre-meeting research briefs so reps walk into each conversation with context.
