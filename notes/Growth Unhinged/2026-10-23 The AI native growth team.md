# The AI native growth team

**Source**: [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/the-ai-native-growth-team)
**Author**: Kyle Poyar (featuring Kameron Tanseli) | **Date**: Oct 23, 2026

---

## Summary

Kyle Poyar profiles Fyxer's AI-native growth engineering team, which ran 514 experiments in 12 months and grew from $1M to $30M ARR. The article argues that small, fast experiments owned end to end by growth engineers, supported by AI tools, can drive outsized revenue growth.

## Key Takeaways

- **Test paywall timing early:** Fyxer moved a credit card prompt before the free trial, lifting trial-to-paid conversion from 5% to 35%. Paying customers doubled even though signups dipped, so measure total paying customers, not just conversion rate.
- **Show the effective monthly price on annual plans:** Defaulting to yearly and displaying a 25% discount as the price per month raised the share of annual trials 2.3x. Half of Fyxer's paying customers now choose annual plans.
- **Segment trial length by user type:** Personal-email signups got 14-day trials and everyone else got 7 days, raising personal-user trial starts from 13.4% to 22.1%. Avoid 3-day trials, which caused immediate cancellations.
- **Reduce card-entry anxiety with a timeline:** Copy Canva's checkout approach by showing exactly what happens today, in 5 days, and in 7 days, and send an email reminder before the first charge.
- **Run a high volume of small experiments with AI support:** Fyxer's four-engineer core team ran about 90 experiments each per year, using Claude Code to automate analysis and tools like Codex to ship small backend changes. Give growth engineers end-to-end ownership of outcomes, not just tasks.
