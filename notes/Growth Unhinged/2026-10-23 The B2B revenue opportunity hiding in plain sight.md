# The B2B revenue opportunity hiding in plain sight

**Source**: [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/the-personal-email-opportunity)
**Author**: Kyle Poyar | **Date**: Oct 23, 2026

---

## Summary

Personal email signups have become a major, undervalued source of B2B pipeline, especially for AI-native products, because enrichment tools can now de-anonymize them far more accurately and cheaply than before. Teams that stop blocking or excluding personal emails can uncover enterprise buyers already trialing their product.

## Key Takeaways

- **Stop excluding personal emails from reporting.** Treat them as potential pipeline by enriching them rather than blocking signups or dropping them from funnel metrics.
- **Run enrichment on personal email signups now.** Enrichment coverage and unit economics have improved sharply, so the cost can be justified by the value of the few high-fit enterprise users.
- **Prioritize enrichment for enterprise-fit signups.** Use company-level signals to route the users most likely from large organizations into targeted GTM plays before competitors reach them.
- **Plan for AI churn by converting trialists early.** Since users often test several products in a category, identify and engage best-fit prospects quickly to win them as stickier, higher-value B2B customers.
- **Expect personal emails to be a large share of signups.** AI-native products often see 75-90% personal email signups, so build the process into your funnel rather than treating it as an edge case.
