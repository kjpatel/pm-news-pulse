# The 30-day upsell window

**Source**: [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/the-30-day-upsell-window)
**Author**: Kyle Poyar | **Date**: Sep 30, 2026

---

## Summary

Analysis of 10.5 million purchases across 4,029 SaaS and AI-native companies finds that customers are up to 10x more receptive to upsells in their first 30 days than in months 4-11. Timing matters most for seat upgrades, plan upgrades, and win-backs, with AI-native products recovering churned customers better but expanding seats less than SaaS.

## Key Takeaways

- **Pitch seat expansion in the first month.** Seat upgrade rates peak at 0.83% in month one and fall to 0.09% by month 11, so build invite flows into early onboarding rather than waiting for months 6-12.
- **Trigger invites from product signals.** Prompt users to invite a teammate when they create collaborative work, repeatedly export results, or manually share outputs, and add a Slack integration where teams already work.
- **Bundle plan upgrades with annual renewal.** About 40% of first-year plan upgrades happen at the month-12 renewal, so turn that renewal into a value review that summarizes results and shows what the next tier unlocks.
- **Offer a 30-day trial of the next tier early.** Give new customers a free trial of the higher plan soon after purchase, and show locked features in context with a clear explanation of their value.
- **Run a day 1, 30, and 60 win-back sequence.** Churners reactivate at 2.67% in month one and more than 5% within three months, so segment win-back offers by cancellation reason and prioritize high-intent signals like returning visits or new teammates.
