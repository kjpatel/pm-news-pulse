# What’s working right now in AI search

**Source**: [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/what-s-working-right-now-in-ai-search)
**Author**: Kyle Poyar | **Date**: Sep 15, 2026

---

## Summary

Answer engine optimization (AEO) has become the single biggest channel where B2B marketers are increasing investment in 2026, because AI engines now recommend products and often build buyers' shortlists before they visit a website. The article rounds up tactics from marketers on AEO metrics, shaping what AI says about a product, and YouTube as an emerging citation battleground.

## Key Takeaways

- **Treat AI search as a core acquisition channel:** AI searches likely make up at least a third of all searches when AI Overviews and LLM queries are combined, so budget for AEO rather than waiting to see if it matters.
- **Optimize for the shortlist, not the click:** Buyers increasingly form shortlists inside ChatGPT, Claude, and similar tools before reaching your site, so track whether AI engines name your product in relevant category queries.
- **Audit what AI says about your product:** Test how AI models describe your product and correct or reinforce those descriptions so the recommendations match your positioning.
- **Measure AEO with dedicated metrics:** Build AEO-specific measurement, such as share of AI recommendations, instead of relying only on traditional organic traffic.
- **Evaluate YouTube as an AI citation source:** Consider YouTube content as a source AI engines reference, since the author argues it is a bigger AEO opportunity than Reddit.
