# How the GTM playbook has changed in 2026

**Source**: [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/b2b-gtm-playbook-for-2026)
**Author**: Kyle Poyar | **Date**: Sep 20, 2026

---

## Summary

The B2B go-to-market playbook has shifted more in the past two years than in the prior decade, driven by new AI-era terms and infrastructure. This field guide maps the key motions, AI GTM infrastructure, outbound buying signals, and working pricing models for founders and GTM leaders. The full article is paywalled, so this summary reflects only the publicly visible introduction.

## Key Takeaways

- **Learn the new GTM vocabulary as a signal, not jargon.** Terms like AEO, context engineering, MCP, FDE, and token economics mark real changes in how B2B companies acquire and expand customers.
- **Audit your current motions against the modern channel mix.** Check which acquisition and expansion channels your team relies on, and identify where AI-driven discovery or buying behavior may be changing the funnel.
- **Build the AI GTM infrastructure deliberately.** Decide which data, tooling, and context layers your go-to-market team needs before scaling AI-assisted outbound or content work.
- **Use buying signals to make outbound smarter.** Prioritize prospects by observable intent and trigger events rather than volume-based cold outreach.
- **Revisit your pricing model against what is actually working.** Compare your current pricing structure with the models the article highlights to see whether it still fits how customers buy and use AI products.
