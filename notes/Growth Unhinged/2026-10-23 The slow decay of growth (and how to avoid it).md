# The slow decay of growth (and how to avoid it)

**Source**: [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/the-slow-decay-of-growth-and-how-to-avoid-it)
**Author**: Kyle Poyar | **Date**: Oct 23, 2026

---

## Summary

Software growth rates tend to decay, and most startups fail to hold their growth year over year. Using ChartMogul data and examples of companies that reversed the slide, the article argues that growth endurance can be preserved by increasing GTM velocity, shifting from one-off tactics to recurring campaigns, and building more repeatable channels.

## Key Takeaways

- **Measure growth endurance, not just growth rate.** Track this year's growth divided by last year's to see whether momentum is compounding or decaying, since it strongly predicts valuation multiples.
- **Shorten GTM cycles to a weekly cadence.** Replace quarterly planning with weekly sprints, set a firm SLA for lead follow-up (such as one hour), and tighten each step of the buying process.
- **Turn launches into multi-week campaigns.** Bundle product releases and content under a broader theme with live streams, guides, and public assets so each launch keeps generating momentum for months.
- **Invest in partner-led, co-sell channels.** Build a lightweight referral program with accounting, RevOps, or other complementary partners, since partner-sourced deals can close faster and retain better than cold outbound.
- **Focus spend on the channels that work at your stage.** Double down on LinkedIn, warm outbound, and founder brand under $1M ARR, and add intent-based outbound as you move into the $1-10M range.
