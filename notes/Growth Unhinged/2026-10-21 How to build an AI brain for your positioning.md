# How to build an AI brain for your positioning

**Source**: [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/ai-brain-for-your-positioning)
**Author**: Kyle Poyar (featuring Melissa Kwan) | **Date**: Oct 21, 2026

---

## Summary

Melissa Kwan, co-founder of eWebinar, explains how she diagnosed a product-messaging fit problem and repositioned her company by building a Claude-powered 'AI brain' that holds customer quotes, competitor research, old copy, and positioning frameworks. The core argument is that positioning is a company-wide retraining effort, and an AI knowledge base can make it feasible without an agency or marketing team.

## Key Takeaways

- **Diagnose messaging fit before fixing anything else.** Get on sales calls and ask prospects why they haven't moved forward; if they can't explain what you do in a few seconds, the problem is positioning, not SEO, ads, or sales.
- **Treat repositioning as a full-surface change.** Update the homepage, subpages, onboarding and nurture emails, directory listings, demo scripts, in-product copy, and call scripts together, so new customers don't get the old framing after signing up.
- **Build a single AI project as your positioning source of truth.** Load it with call transcripts, tagged customer quotes, old copy as a 'what not to do' reference, competitor cheat sheets, your target messaging, and the frameworks you trust, then generate content from it.
- **Collect customer quotes systematically from day one.** Tag every positive comment so it's searchable; Melissa's 650+ quotes from six years made it possible to ground the AI in real customer language.
- **Map competitors as substitute behaviors, not just software.** Write a cheat sheet for each alternative (such as live Zoom or a YouTube video) covering why people choose it, why they choose you, where you fall short, and the objections that come up.
