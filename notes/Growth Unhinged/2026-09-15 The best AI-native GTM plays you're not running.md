# The best AI-native GTM plays you're not running

**Source**: [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/the-best-ai-native-gtm-plays-you-re-not-running)
**Author**: Kyle Poyar (featuring Brendan Short) | **Date**: Sep 15, 2026

---

## Summary

Outbound is regaining momentum because better data and automation make it more effective, and AI has shifted GTM from static 'plays' to continuously running systems. The authors show how five AI-native workflows, mixing deterministic and agentic steps, outperform their 2025 versions.

## Key Takeaways

- **Make re-engagement timing dynamic, not calendar-based.** Instead of reaching out to every closed-lost deal after 9 months, trigger outreach when a cluster of signals appears, such as a leadership change, new funding, a relevant job posting, or the departure of the champion who killed the deal.
- **Personalize win-back emails with the real reason the deal died.** Have an agent pull the last call transcript and extract the specific objection, like a competitor's feature gap or a budget freeze from a reorg, then reference what has changed since.
- **Route emails by account tier.** Send Tier 1 re-engagement drafts to a rep for review, and let Tier 2 and below send automatically to keep volume up without sacrificing quality on high-value accounts.
- **Layer plays on each other.** Combine closed-lost re-engagement with champion tracking so the agent knows whether the person who killed the deal is still at the company and adjusts the approach accordingly.
- **Mix deterministic and agentic steps deliberately.** Use if-then logic for predictable actions and reserve autonomous AI steps for research and drafting, and document the tool chain (CRM, signal sources, transcript APIs, enrichment, LLM drafting, sequencing) so the system is maintainable.
