# 5 GTM plays working right now

**Source**: [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/5-gtm-plays-working-right-now)
**Author**: Kyle Poyar | **Date**: Nov 11, 2026

---

## Summary

This article profiles five B2B growth plays from 2026 at Clay, Granola, incident.io, Lovable, and Vercel, showing how each company used AI automation, creator-led distribution, better attribution, events, or community to grow. The core argument is that these concrete, operator-tested tactics are working right now.

## Key Takeaways

- **Build an internal autonomous ad workflow before paying agency fees.** incident.io built a Claude skill that creates, manages, and pauses LinkedIn, Google, Meta, and X campaigns, drawing on an internal MCP server, CRM, and intent signals. Start with approved messaging and benchmarks, then add guardrails and a weekly self-review loop.
- **Turn organic LinkedIn mentions into a managed ambassador program.** Granola recruits operator-influencers (CMOs, founders, engineers) through enrichment of existing users and daily scans of LinkedIn mentions. It pays for reach, content ideas, and boostable ad potential, and its thought-leader ads earn about 10x the engagement of other LinkedIn ads.
- **Fix attribution before judging which GTM plays work.** Clay's growth lead combined CRM, product data, call recordings, and self-reported 'how did you hear about us' data with AI agents to build a unified attribution view. Without this, teams can't agree on which channels deserve more budget.
- **Scale events by systematizing what already converts.** Vercel tripled its high-converting IRL event program, so focus on the formats and audiences that already produce pipeline before expanding the calendar.
- **Use hackathons to generate word-of-mouth and product proof.** Lovable turned a hackathon into a growth channel by producing shareable builds that users spread, so consider structuring an event around customer-built outputs that others want to share.
