# What’s working to improve free-to-paid conversion

**Source**: [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/how-to-improve-free-to-paid-conversion)
**Author**: Kyle Poyar | **Date**: Oct 23, 2026

---

## Summary

Free-to-paid conversion is one of the highest-leverage growth levers, and the biggest gains usually come from attracting better-fit users, speeding up time to value, and fixing pricing and human touchpoints rather than changing the product alone. Survey data from 200 B2B software products backs up these tactics, most of which need no extra marketing spend or headcount.

## Key Takeaways

- **Shift acquisition toward high-intent channels.** Organic search, referrals, social, and LLM traffic convert best, while paid marketing converts worst, so expand organic efforts and tighten paid targeting around your best-converting users.
- **Reverse-engineer your highest-converting users.** Compare conversion and LTV by segment (for example, work email versus personal email signups) and use those traits to refine ad targeting and ICP definitions.
- **Cut onboarding friction and raise motivation.** Shorten onboarding, tailor it to use cases, add AI-assisted setup, and use interactive demos or onboarding rewards, then test each change because what works for one product may not transfer to another.
- **Assign a single owner for conversion.** Product teams often own activation but are not accountable for free-to-paid conversion, which usually sits with sales or growth, so name one owner who is measured on the number.
- **Test pricing and packaging as a conversion lever.** Pricing and packaging was the most-cited category among the survey's growth experiments, so clarify plan differences and test plan structure to reduce confusion at the decision point.
