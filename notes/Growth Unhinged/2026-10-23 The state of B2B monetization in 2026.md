# The state of B2B monetization in 2026

**Source**: [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/the-state-of-b2b-monetization-in-2026)
**Author**: Kyle Poyar | **Date**: Oct 23, 2026

---

## Summary

Based on a survey of 230+ B2B software and AI companies, the article argues that AI is pushing pricing away from per-seat subscriptions toward hybrid models that charge for work delivered by software and agents. Companies are changing pricing rapidly, yet nobody is satisfied, mainly because expansion revenue is lacking.

## Key Takeaways

- **Consider a hybrid pricing model.** It is now the most common model (37% adoption, up from 25% a year earlier), combining a base subscription with usage or AI consumption on top.
- **Add a premium edition to build expansion revenue.** Price it around 50-100% above the base edition, since not enough expansion revenue is the most frequently cited pricing challenge.
- **Layer in consumption limits for heavy AI users.** Power users drive most token consumption (the author's experience is 70%+ from the top 10%), so limits and add-ons can protect margins.
- **Plan for AI margins well below SaaS margins.** AI margins are around 50% versus 70-80%+ for SaaS, so model unit costs before committing to flat-rate AI features.
- **Expect AI credits and further pricing changes within 6-12 months.** A third of companies plan to introduce AI credits, and about half of companies above $50M ARR plan to do so this year, but the author warns that credits can become hard for customers to manage across vendors.
