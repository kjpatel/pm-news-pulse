# How to use Claude Code as a GTM leader

**Source**: [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/how-to-use-claude-code-as-a-gtm-leader)
**Author**: Kyle Poyar (featuring Dee Kapila) | **Date**: Sep 15, 2026

---

## Summary

Fin's senior director of customer success, Dee Kapila, shows how her GTM and customer success teams became AI-first by giving non-engineers unrestricted access to Claude through an internal data layer, then running a hands-on enablement program. She also shares a Claude Code workflow that cut a two-hour weekly task she had been doing every Sunday.

## Key Takeaways

- **Remove usage limits during initial adoption.** Give non-technical teams unrestricted access to the tools and emphasize hands-on building, since quantity of experiments matters more than polish at this stage.
- **Run a two-hour hands-on master class before anything else.** Set prerequisites in advance (access, basic command line), block the calendar, spend most of the session building, and follow up with recurring office hours across all time zones.
- **Measure adoption by depth, not logins.** Build an adoption report that tracks layered usage dimensions and publishes team builds, and make every all-hands showcase something someone built.
- **Set standards as the team matures.** Define best practices, sanctioned skills, and provenance checks, and reshape relationships with Operations and Data teams as the team becomes a power user.
- **Automate your own recurring pain first.** Pick a repetitive weekly task you personally dread, such as a two-hour Sunday routine, and rebuild it in Claude Code to prove value and model the behavior for your team.
