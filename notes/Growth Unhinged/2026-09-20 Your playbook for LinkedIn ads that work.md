# Your playbook for LinkedIn ads that work

**Source**: [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/linkedin-ad-playbook)
**Author**: Kyle Poyar (featuring Bogdan Liutic) | **Date**: Sep 20, 2026

---

## Summary

LinkedIn ads can work for B2B companies, but only when the economics fit: an LTV of at least $10,000, a decision-maker audience of roughly 10,000 or more, and an ICP that actually spends time on LinkedIn. Even then, ads only win the sales call, so they need a multi-touch flow behind them to close deals.

## Key Takeaways

- **Check your unit economics before spending on LinkedIn ads.** Aim for an LTV or ACV of at least $10,000, since LinkedIn CPMs (about $37 on average) are roughly 3-4x Meta's and only pay off when one deal covers months of impressions.
- **Confirm the decision-maker audience is large enough.** Make sure your TAM at the decision-maker level is around 10,000 people or more; if it is smaller, use direct outreach and events instead of ads.
- **Calculate your LTV:CAC ratio with your own numbers.** Plug in LTV, close rate, show rate, and media budget into a calculator. Treat 3+ as a go, 2-3 as workable, and below 2 as a reason to walk away.
- **Design a multi-touch flow rather than a single ad-to-demo path.** Since senior B2B buyers take six to eight touches before booking a call, combine ads with cold outreach and content instead of relying on ads that send people straight to book a demo.
- **Measure cost per company influenced, not just cost per lead.** LinkedIn has been reported as the social network with the lowest cost per company influenced (about $76) and the only one with a positive return on ad spend, so judge it on pipeline impact.
