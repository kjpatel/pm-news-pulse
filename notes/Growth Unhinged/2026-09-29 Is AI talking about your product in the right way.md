# Is AI talking about your product in the right way?

**Source**: [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/is-ai-talking-about-your-product-in-the-right-way)
**Author**: Kyle Poyar (featuring Casey Hill) | **Date**: Sep 29, 2026

---

## Summary

Websites now need to be optimized for two audiences, humans and AI agents, because LLMs increasingly shape buyer trust and purchase decisions. The article shows early adopters using structural site elements like footers, homepages, and comparison pages to shape what AI says about their products.

## Key Takeaways

- **Feature top customers in your global footer or nav:** Link directly to your strongest customer case studies in site-wide navigation, then track whether prompts like "What notable brands use [company]?" start citing your domain more often.
- **Monitor AI answers for three core questions:** Regularly check whether LLMs name your best customers, pitch your product against competitors, and accurately explain your pricing as customer-friendly.
- **Treat your homepage as a dual-audience asset:** Homepages now account for roughly 24% of ChatGPT referrals, so write clear, structured headers and copy that both LLMs and human visitors can parse.
- **Publish a comparison page against Claude:** Create a head-to-head article comparing your product with Claude so LLMs have a clear, citable source when users ask how the two differ.
- **Structure content with clear headers and linking:** Since LLMs extract headers and summarize the text beneath them, use descriptive section headings and strong internal linking so key themes surface in AI-generated answers.
