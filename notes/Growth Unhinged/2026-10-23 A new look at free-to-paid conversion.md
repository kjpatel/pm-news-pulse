# A new look at free-to-paid conversion

**Source**: [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/free-to-paid-conversion-report)
**Author**: Kyle Poyar | **Date**: Oct 23, 2026

---

## Summary

Kyle Poyar analyzes conversion data from 200 B2B software products to show that free-to-paid conversion varies widely and is one of the highest-leverage growth levers, with free trials dominating over freemium and credit-card requirements sharply lifting conversion. The median rate is 8%, but few products actually hit it.

## Key Takeaways

- **Use a free trial as your primary entry point:** 57% of products use one, more than twice the rate of freemium (26%), and trial-based products are the most common model in the sample.
- **Require a credit card for free trials if you can:** Trials that require a card convert at about 30%, more than 5x the rate of those that don't, though the card requirement will reduce signups.
- **Measure conversion against signups, not just conversion rates:** Free trials look slightly better than freemium on conversion, but the advantage disappears once signup rates are factored in.
- **Benchmark by segment, not the 8% median:** Conversion varies about 10x between the top and bottom 20% of self-serve products, so compare against products with similar ARR and price points.
- **Keep human touchpoints in self-serve motions:** 80% of free trial products engage enterprise users when they enter a trial, which suggests self-serve and sales-assisted paths work best together.
