# The new battleground in GTM

**Source**: [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/the-new-battleground-in-gtm)
**Author**: Kyle Poyar | **Date**: Sep 22, 2026

---

## Summary

Third-party intent signals have become a commodity as every competitor buys the same data, so GTM teams need to combine third-party signals with their own first-party data (champions, closed-lost deals, product usage) and use the external data only to decide when to act. The moat comes from how creatively the combined data is used.

## Key Takeaways

- **Wait about a month before reaching out to a champion who changes jobs:** New executives are overwhelmed in their first weeks, so pause, then reach out by phone and email referencing their past product usage.
- **Re-engage closed-lost deals on signals, not a 9-month reminder:** Pair each lost opp's loss reason with a matching trigger, such as a funding event or CFO change for a budget loss, and only reactivate when that signal appears.
- **Track which signals actually overcome specific loss reasons:** Build a learning loop that tests whether leadership changes, new strategic initiatives, or tech stack changes reopen deals, and adjust timing, targeting, and messaging based on results.
- **Narrow website visitor retargeting to target accounts:** De-anonymize visitors on high-value pages, qualify their account tier, and only spend ad dollars on buying-group contacts at accounts you actually want, instead of retargeting everyone for 30 days.
- **Add custom first-party signals to your outreach triggers:** Use signals unique to your product, such as a prospect's free trial or product usage, and add VIP touches like sending follow-ups from the CEO or founder to lift reply rates.
