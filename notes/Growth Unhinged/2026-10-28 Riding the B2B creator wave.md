# Riding the B2B creator wave

**Source**: [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/riding-the-b2b-creator-wave)
**Author**: Kyle Poyar | **Date**: Oct 28, 2026

---

## Summary

B2B newsletter creators have gained pricing power as brands compete for attention in a channel that readers actively consume and trust. The article argues that brands and creators can both win by matching audience size to goals, diversifying ad mixes, and ensuring the offer and audience fit.

## Key Takeaways

- **Segment creators by audience size:** Use small creators (2,500-10,000 subs) for scalable, programmatic ROI, a few medium creators (10,000-40,000) that closely fit your niche, and select large creators (40,000+) for reach.
- **Prioritize audience-ICP fit over raw reach:** Check company size, role, location, and seniority against the newsletter's readers, and judge engagement through open rates, click-through rates, and website views.
- **Pick offers that match the reader's stage:** Promote a low-friction action such as a report download or webinar signup rather than expecting an enterprise purchase from a single newsletter ad.
- **Place ads in top sections and include your logo:** Creators should aim for the top three sections of the newsletter, and brands should use logos, since these tactics are cited as improving performance.
- **Test small or medium creators before committing to large ones:** Large creators often sell out three months ahead, so validate the channel with smaller partners first, then scale spend to proven winners.
