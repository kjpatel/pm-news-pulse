# How to measure the impact of AI search the right way

**Source**: [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/how-to-measure-the-impact-of-ai-search-the-right-way)
**Author**: Kyle Poyar (featuring Kevin Indig) | **Date**: Sep 15, 2026

---

## Summary

Traffic-based metrics badly undercount the impact of AI search (AEO) because users rarely click AI citations and most AI referral traffic shows up as direct. Kevin Indig argues teams should measure AI visibility with a ladder of leading indicators, quality guardrails, and lagging revenue indicators, using a frozen prompt set and a regular reporting cadence.

## Key Takeaways

- **Freeze a set of 20 to 50 high-intent prompts** across personas, use cases, and buying stages for at least four weeks so you measure real change rather than prompt drift.
- **Log every AI answer run** with the prompt, model, location, answer text, cited URLs, brands mentioned, and shortlist position to create the raw data table for all measurement.
- **Run measurement on two clocks:** check signal quality weekly (crawler access, retrieval and citation share, accuracy of product descriptions) and check business allocation monthly (opportunities, sales mentions, win rate, revenue).
- **Stop using last-click traffic as your AEO scorecard,** since AI referrals are tiny and about 70% land as Direct in GA4; instead track movement up the visibility ladder and connect it to downstream pipeline.
- **Report to the board as movement across the ladder, not a single AEO score,** using one slide that shows what changed in leading indicators, whether quality improved, what moved downstream, and what you will change next month.
