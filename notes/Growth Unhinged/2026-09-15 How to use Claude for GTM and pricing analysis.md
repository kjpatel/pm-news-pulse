# How to use Claude for GTM and pricing analysis

**Source**: [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/claude-skills-gtm-and-pricing)
**Author**: Kyle Poyar | **Date**: Sep 15, 2026

---

## Summary

Kyle Poyar argues that his 15 years of GTM expertise can be packaged as reusable Claude skills, so that frameworks from years of newsletter editions can be applied on demand rather than read. He shares several skills, including deep GTM research, pricing teardown, ICP sharpening, and GTM play brainstorming, and explains how to build and install them.

## Key Takeaways

- **Build a research skill first.** Point Claude (Cowork or Chat) at a curated methodology source and have it generate a SKILL.md file, since a solid research skill becomes the foundation for other GTM workflows.
- **Require a research plan before execution.** Have the deep research skill gather context and propose a plan for your sign-off, prioritize primary sources, and deliver a cited, pyramid-structured report, which avoids over-indexing on single blog posts or Reddit threads.
- **Audit your pricing page for both humans and AI agents.** Run a pricing teardown that scores 10 dimensions, including transparency, plan clarity, and machine-readable pricing, since LLMs increasingly recommend tools and opaque pricing can make you invisible or misrepresented.
- **Run the pricing teardown through Claude Chat, not Cowork.** Chat has better browsing and scraping capabilities, which matters if you are on a PC without the Chrome Connector.
- **Customize skills to your business and iterate.** Upload skill files via Customize → Skills, then edit them based on feedback so the outputs fit your context and preferences.
