# Nobody cares about your new product (sorry)

**Source**: [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/product-launch-playbook)
**Author**: Kyle Poyar (featuring John Hurley, Ben Pollack, and Trevor Pyle) | **Date**: Sep 29, 2026

---

## Summary

Product release velocity is accelerating because AI coding tools have compressed shipping timelines, which makes it harder for customers to notice any single launch. Rippling, Notion, and Profound treat product releases as a deliberate growth lever rather than a routine announcement. (This summary is based on the free preview; the full playbooks are behind a paywall.)

## Key Takeaways

- **Treat each launch as a growth campaign, not an announcement.** Plan marketing, sales, and CS activation around each release, since teams competing for attention otherwise struggle to get noticed.
- **Coordinate launches across go-to-market teams early.** Marketing, sales, CS, and customers all need to be prepared before release, because they are the ones who must keep up with the pace of new features.
- **Prioritize releases that open new revenue lines.** Rippling's 121% AI revenue growth came from specific new products like an AI analyst, spend console, MCP, and agent identity management, so pick launches that can create distinct SKUs or use cases.
- **Use a launch to reposition the company.** Profound moved from AI prompt tracking to an agentic marketing platform, showing that a product release can signal a broader strategic shift to the market.
- **Study the playbooks from the marketing leaders who ran them.** Learn directly from Notion's, Rippling's, and Profound's marketing leaders to adapt their launch sequencing to your own product.
