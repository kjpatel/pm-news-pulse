# Is this the last place to automate?

**Source**: [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/sales-frameworks-sam-mckenna)
**Author**: Kyle Poyar (featuring Sam McKenna) | **Date**: Sep 23, 2026

---

## Summary

AI-generated outreach has lost its edge because buyers are numb to it, so sales teams should keep the human effort in the places AI can't fake, like personal research, authentic connection, and well-timed, buyer-focused outreach. Sam McKenna's frameworks show that effort, not automation, is the differentiator.

## Key Takeaways

- **Use AI for research, not the email itself.** Have AI surface executive citations, conference talks, and company themes, then write the outreach yourself using those real insights.
- **Structure cold emails around a genuine connection.** Open with something you actually found and can relate to, bridge to a buyer-focused challenge, name a specific role-level problem, preempt the likely objection, and close with an easy reply invitation.
- **Batch-write on Monday through Wednesday and send on Thursday and Friday.** Draft about 50 researched emails, send half on Thursday and half on Friday to avoid the Monday inbox crush, and reach executives when they're thinking about their week.
- **Send a short follow-up within 48 hours, including on weekends.** Keep it to a line or two asking whether they read the earlier email, since executives often check messages on weekends.
- **Never send a calendar link; offer open time blocks instead.** Propose wide windows that include movable internal meetings so you control the timing and take scheduling work off the buyer's plate.
