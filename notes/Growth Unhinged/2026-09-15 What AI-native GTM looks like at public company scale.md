# What AI-native GTM looks like at public company scale

**Source**: [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/what-ai-native-gtm-looks-like-at-public-company-scale)
**Author**: Kyle Poyar (featuring Oran Akron) | **Date**: Sep 15, 2026

---

## Summary

monday.com's internal RevAI team used AI agents to rethink go-to-market workflows at enterprise scale, generating millions of dollars in pipeline and cutting demo request response times from 24 hours to under 2 minutes. The article argues that the real challenge is making AI GTM work amid complex routing, global rollouts, legacy stacks, and compliance requirements.

## Key Takeaways

- **Shrink lead response time to minutes:** Route inbound demo requests to AI agents so prospects get a response in under 2 minutes instead of 24 hours.
- **Start with a few high-impact GTM workflows:** Pick specific workflows where agents can book meetings and create pipeline, rather than trying to automate the whole GTM motion at once.
- **Plan for routing logic and compliance early:** Map territory rules, regional requirements, and data policies before deploying agents, since these are where enterprise AI GTM efforts tend to break.
- **Expect to rebuild agents at enterprise scale:** Budget for iteration, because agents that work in a pilot often need redesign once they meet global rollouts and legacy systems.
- **Assign a dedicated owner for AI GTM:** Follow monday.com's model of a central AI-for-GTM leader running an internal team to redesign workflows with agents.
