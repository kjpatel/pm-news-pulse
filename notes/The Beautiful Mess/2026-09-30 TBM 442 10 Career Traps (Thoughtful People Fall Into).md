# TBM 442: 10 Career Traps (Thoughtful People Fall Into)

**Source**: [The Beautiful Mess](https://cutlefish.substack.com/p/tbm-442-10-career-traps-thoughtful)
**Author**: John Cutler | **Date**: Sep 30, 2026

---

## Summary

The article identifies ten common career traps that thoughtful, capable people tend to fall into, framing them as pitfalls that can quietly stall professional growth. The provided text is only an introduction, so the specific traps are not detailed here.

## Key Takeaways

- **Audit your career for autopilot choices.** Review the last year of role, project, and team decisions and ask which ones you made deliberately versus by default.
- **Watch for thoughtfulness used as an excuse to avoid risk.** Identify one visible, low-cost move (a proposal, a demo, a stretch assignment) you have been postponing while you analyze it.
- **Get explicit feedback on how your work is perceived.** Ask your manager and two peers what they see as your strengths and blind spots, and compare the answers with your own view.
- **Invest in your manager relationship as a career asset.** Schedule a recurring conversation focused on your growth goals, not only status updates.
- **Define success in your own terms before you chase someone else's ladder.** Write down the outcomes you want in the next 12 to 24 months and check each opportunity against them.
