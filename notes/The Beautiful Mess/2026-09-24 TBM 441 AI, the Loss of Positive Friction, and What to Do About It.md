## Summary

The article, TBM 441, appears to argue that AI is removing positive friction from product work and that teams need to deliberately decide what friction to keep. The provided text is cut off after its opening line, so this summary is inferred from the title.

## Key Takeaways

- **Identify which friction in your product workflow is productive** by listing the steps where slowing down improves judgment, such as debate, review, or discovery, before automating them away.
- **Protect deliberate pauses in AI-assisted work** by building explicit review checkpoints into workflows where speed could hide weak reasoning.
- **Audit where AI removes learning** by asking which tasks teams used to struggle through that built product understanding, and decide whether to reintroduce them.
- **Redefine productivity beyond output volume** by measuring outcomes such as decision quality and customer learning instead of how much work AI accelerates.
