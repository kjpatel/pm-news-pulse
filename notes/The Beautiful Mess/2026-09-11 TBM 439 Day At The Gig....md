# TBM 439: Day At The Gig...

**Source**: [The Beautiful Mess](https://cutlefish.substack.com/p/tbm-439-day-at-the-gig)
**Author**: John Cutler | **Date**: Sep 11, 2026

---

## Summary

The author recently started a new job and is spending most of his time learning how everything works: the product, workflows, history, people, artifacts, and the context the team has built up over time. The implicit argument is that absorbing this accumulated context is the central task when joining a new team.

## Key Takeaways

- **Treat the first weeks as a discovery phase.** Set aside deliberate time to learn the product, workflows, and history rather than jumping straight into delivery.
- **Map the people and their context.** Identify who holds institutional knowledge and schedule short conversations to learn how they work and what they have seen.
- **Read the existing artifacts before proposing change.** Go through docs, decision records, roadmaps, and tickets to understand why things are the way they are.
- **Ask about history, not just current state.** Questions like 'what did we try before?' and 'what did we learn from that?' reveal context that current documents rarely capture.
- **Write down what you learn.** Keeping your own running notes on the product, processes, and people makes the accumulated context easier to share and to test your understanding against.
