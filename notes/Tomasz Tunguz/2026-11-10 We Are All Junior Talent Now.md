# We Are All Junior Talent Now

**Source**: [Tomasz Tunguz](https://tomtunguz.com/we-are-all-junior-talent-now/)
**Author**: Tomasz Tunguz (featuring KC) | **Date**: Nov 10, 2026

---

## Summary

Companies face a choice between using AI to do the same tasks faster, which lifts earnings, or reimagining what is possible, which compounds into explosive separation. As experience fades quickly, the differentiator for people becomes emotional intelligence and how they show up as humans.

## Key Takeaways

- **Drop the junior/senior labels.** Relevant experience decays fast, so evaluate people on what they know now and how quickly they learn, not on tenure or past titles.
- **Add 'agent manager' to every role.** Reframe each job title as managing agents that do part of the work, such as a sales agent manager, to clarify how work is changing.
- **Move system updating from humans to agents.** Stop having people act as middleware that does the work and then logs it, so humans can focus on working with other humans.
- **Invest in emotional intelligence.** Build hiring, feedback, and promotion criteria around integrity, humility, and a willingness to learn, since these are what separate people when everyone has access to strong AI.
- **Pursue reimagination over efficiency.** Use AI to ask what new things the business could do, rather than only cutting time from existing tasks, to avoid the slow-growth path.
