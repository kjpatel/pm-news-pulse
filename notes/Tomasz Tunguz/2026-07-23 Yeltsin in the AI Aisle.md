## Summary

The AI inference market is rapidly segmenting across cost, speed, and accuracy dimensions, with older open-source models still commanding significant traffic alongside frontier models, and mixture-of-experts architectures bringing frontier-class quality to local computing.

## Key Takeaways

- **Monitor market segmentation dynamics**: Track which models dominate different inference tiers (local, mid-market, frontier) as competition creates specialized solutions rather than one-size-fits-all offerings.
- **Evaluate mixture-of-experts architecture**: Test MoE models like Laguna S 2.1 that activate only a fraction of parameters during inference, achieving frontier-level quality at local compute costs.
- **Measure domain-specific performance**: Move beyond benchmark scores and test tool-call failure rates and error reduction in your actual production workloads to justify model migrations.
- **Expect continued tier compression**: Plan for ongoing improvements in local and mid-market model quality as competition pushes better architectures down the cost/performance curve.
