# The Third Product-Market Fit in AI

**Source**: [Tomasz Tunguz](https://tomtunguz.com/the-third-product-market-fit-in-ai/)
**Author**: Tomasz Tunguz | **Date**: Oct 26, 2026

---

## Summary

Black October's wave of security vulnerabilities, including attacks on banks, certificate authorities, and open source libraries, marks the third AI product-market fit, this time in security. A survey of 75 enterprise security practitioners suggests AI-driven security budgets will grow sharply, because AI can defend as well as attack, provided organizations fund the tokens.

## Key Takeaways

- **Prioritize AI for SOC triage.** Alert volume and the shrinking response window, from months to minutes, make human-only triage unworkable, so evaluate AI tools that automate alert analysis first.
- **Use natural-language querying across security data.** Letting analysts query SIEM stores in plain language turns multi-day ticket queues into answers in seconds.
- **Run continuous AI-driven penetration testing.** Models can probe every endpoint at a fraction of the cost of a human red team, so test your systems on a continuous basis rather than annually.
- **Rank vulnerabilities by reachability and exploitability.** Scanners report far more findings than teams can patch, so use AI to focus remediation on the flaws attackers can actually reach.
- **Budget for token consumption in security.** Every security category consumes significant tokens, so security budgets should account for AI compute costs and could eventually outstrip software spend.
