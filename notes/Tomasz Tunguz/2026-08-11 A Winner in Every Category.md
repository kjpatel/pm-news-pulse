# A Winner in Every Category

**Source**: [Tomasz Tunguz](https://www.tomtunguz.com/a-winner-in-every-category/)
**Author**: Tomasz Tunguz | **Date**: Aug 11, 2026

---

## Summary

Despite historic collapses in SaaS valuations, category leaders like CrowdStrike, Cloudflare, and Shopify command massive premiums to peers by positioning themselves as essential infrastructure for AI agents and agentic workflows.

## Key Takeaways

- **Identify the AI narrative**: Winners trade at 3-8x premiums by clearly articulating how AI agents create new revenue streams (e.g., CrowdStrike's threat detection for agent endpoints, Cloudflare's routing of non-human traffic).
- **Build defensible training data moats**: Samsara commands 10.8x multiples because its 25 trillion annual data points and visual dashcam data can't be replicated—focus on proprietary datasets agents can't access elsewhere.
- **Govern, don't commoditize**: ServiceNow's $1B AI ACV premium comes from being the control layer for enterprise agents—position your product as the governance layer, not just the tool.
- **Capture inference at your take rate**: Shopify's 8.1x multiple reflects agents writing orders directly into their platform at existing commission rates—design for agent-native workflows that land at your existing pricing.
- **Growth alone doesn't justify valuation**: CrowdStrike trades at 34.4x while growing slower than Rubrik (23% vs 46%)—the market values the *durability and scope* of the AI story, not just growth rate.
