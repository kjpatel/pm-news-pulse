# Inference Is the Most Important Market in Software

**Source**: [Tomasz Tunguz](https://tomtunguz.com/inference-is-the-most-important-market-in-software/)
**Author**: Tomasz Tunguz | **Date**: Oct 05, 2026

---

## Summary

AI inference is poised to become the most important software market by 2027, surpassing databases and fundamentally restructuring application economics through massive usage-based costs, compressed gross margins, and infrastructure dominance of COGS.

## Key Takeaways

- **Forecast inference market growth**: Plan for AI inference to reach ~$350B by 2027, making it critical to model how inference costs will comprise a significant portion of your COGS and revenue allocation.
- **Restructure unit economics**: Shift from traditional SaaS margins (72%) to lower gross margins by developing proprietary harnesses to compress token overhead, or face margin compression as you become an inference reseller.
- **Prepare for usage-based revenue models**: Inference consumption bills will exceed platform fees, requiring changes to AE compensation, predictable pricing plans for budget-conscious customers, and revised forecasting methodologies.
- **Optimize for token efficiency**: Implement smaller models, better harnesses, and smart routing techniques to hunt for inference cost efficiencies, similar to how trucking companies optimize fuel consumption.
- **Account for BYOK customer trade-offs**: When enterprises bring their own keys (GPU clusters or model credentials), expect to trade top-line revenue for higher gross margins, requiring adjusted customer acquisition strategies.
