# Honestly, Who Buys SOTA?

**Source**: [Tomasz Tunguz](https://www.tomtunguz.com/model-release-exhaustion/)
**Author**: Tomasz Tunguz | **Date**: Aug 14, 2026

---

## Summary

While state-of-the-art AI models improve rapidly, 84% of tokens on OpenRouter use non-frontier models that deliver 77% of frontier performance at 2.5% of the cost, suggesting price elasticity is reshaping AI model economics and limiting SOTA adoption.

## Key Takeaways

- **Monitor price elasticity trends**: Six non-frontier models capturing 80% of volume at $0.50/m tokens versus $20/m for Fable 5 indicates users prioritize cost-performance tradeoffs over pure capability, requiring vendors to reconsider pricing strategies for market share.
- **Plan for consolidation dynamics**: Enterprise contracts will concentrate on one or two vendors post-SOTA release, meaning each new frontier model release captures progressively less share as "good enough" models entrench despite performance gaps closing.
- **Optimize for deployment economics**: Application companies default to smaller, fine-tuned, and open-source models on a different Pareto frontier (price over performance), not the frontier-performance frontier, fundamentally changing which models win in production.
- **Identify SOTA's true use cases**: State-of-the-art models only justify their premium in specific domains like software engineering architecture and security design where maximum capability directly earns its cost; application builders should evaluate workload-specific requirements rather than default to frontier.
