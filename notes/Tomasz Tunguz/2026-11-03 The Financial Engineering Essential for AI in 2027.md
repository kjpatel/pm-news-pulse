# The Financial Engineering Essential for AI in 2027

**Source**: [Tomasz Tunguz](https://tomtunguz.com/the-financial-engineering-essential-for-ai-in-2027/)
**Author**: Tomasz Tunguz | **Date**: Nov 03, 2026

---

## Summary

Anthropic's updated S-1/A shows GPU rental prices surging, which roughly doubled the cost of compute and turned a quarterly operating profit into a loss. Tunguz argues that in 2027 the winning AI labs will be those that match the duration of their customer contracts to the duration of their compute leases.

## Key Takeaways

- **Match contract length to compute length.** Sell multi-year minimum commitments with locked-in rates to enterprise buyers so revenue duration matches the GPU leases underneath it.
- **Secure long-term capacity before spot prices spike.** Lock in multi-year GPU contracts at fixed rates, since short-term burst capacity can cost several times more and can erase operating profit.
- **Plan for a doubling of unit costs.** A doubling of GPU cost must be offset by model efficiency gains, higher prices, or both, so model cost-per-task improvements should be treated as a margin requirement.
- **Avoid raising token prices in a price war.** Any lab that increases rates in a competitive market cedes share, so margin protection has to come from efficiency and contract structure instead.
- **Account for data center delays in capacity planning.** With 40% to 50% of data center projects delayed and multi-year lead times for power equipment, assume constrained supply will persist and build roadmaps around it.
