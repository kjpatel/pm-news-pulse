# The Fastest Growing Market in Tech isn't AI

**Source**: [Tomasz Tunguz](https://tomtunguz.com/allium-bloomberg-rwa/)
**Author**: Tomasz Tunguz | **Date**: Sep 30, 2026

---

## Summary

Tokenized real-world assets (RWA) trading on blockchains are growing at 53% monthly—faster than AI leaders like Anthropic—and have become central to global finance with major institutions and Bloomberg adopting blockchain-based trading infrastructure.

## Key Takeaways

- **Track RWA adoption metrics** - Monitor monthly growth rates (currently 53%), spot volume, and trade counts to identify emerging financial infrastructure shifts beyond traditional AI metrics.
- **Recognize pre-IPO pricing disruption** - RWA perpetuals now enable price discovery weeks before public listings (SpaceX, Anthropic, OpenAI), fundamentally changing IPO dynamics and investor access.
- **Monitor stablecoin concentration** - Stablecoins are now top-10 US debt holders, exceeding China and Norway—this concentration presents both opportunity and systemic risk worth tracking.
- **Watch institutional blockchain adoption** - JPMorgan, BlackRock, Goldman Sachs, and Bloomberg entering RWA markets signals the infrastructure shift is no longer speculative but mainstream finance.
- **Position data infrastructure plays** - Allium powering Bloomberg's RWA dashboard demonstrates that blockchain data analytics will be as critical as the tokenization itself.
