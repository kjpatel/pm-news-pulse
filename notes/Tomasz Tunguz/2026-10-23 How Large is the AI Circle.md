# How Large is the AI Circle?

**Source**: [Tomasz Tunguz](https://tomtunguz.com/how-large-is-the-ai-circle/)
**Author**: Tomasz Tunguz | **Date**: Oct 23, 2026

---

## Summary

The article argues that AI is following the historical pattern of technology build-outs, with infrastructure spending preceding application revenue, but it is closing the gap about twice as fast as the telecom-era internet did. It also raises the risk that much of the spending is circular, with venture-backed firms funding the AI demand that justifies the infrastructure.

## Key Takeaways

- **Track the infrastructure-to-application revenue gap.** Use Carlota Perez's two-phase model to measure whether application revenue is catching up to capex, since AI passed the 10% mark in about four years versus seven for the telecom build-out.
- **Pressure-test circularity before betting on AI demand.** Check what share of a customer or market's AI spend comes from venture-backed firms, because those dollars may be recycled investment rather than durable end-customer revenue.
- **Model payback using contract rates and cash costs, not list prices.** The article's GPU payback math shows results shift from about 1.2 years to under 1 year depending on contract rate, spot price, and tax treatment, so run sensitivity analyses on each assumption.
- **Benchmark venture funding against a historical base.** Compare cumulative investment across equal elapsed periods (such as the first four years of each era) rather than full decades, to avoid misleading multiples when assessing a boom.
- **Question the source and scope of any spending data.** Recognize that card and bill-pay datasets exclude free tools and personal-account usage, which the article estimates at roughly 40% of AI application spend, so adjust your conclusions accordingly.
