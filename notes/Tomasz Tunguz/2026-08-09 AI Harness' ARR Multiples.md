# AI Harness' ARR Multiples

**Source**: [Tomasz Tunguz](https://www.tomtunguz.com/ai-harness-arr-multiples/)
**Author**: Tomasz Tunguz | **Date**: Aug 09, 2026

---

## Summary

AI harness companies crossing $100M ARR are commanding dramatically varying valuations (25x-125x revenue) despite similar growth rates, with valuation multiples tracking market category position rather than growth speed.

## Key Takeaways

- **Monitor valuation bands** - AI harness companies trade within consistent multiple bands (1-2 bands per company) despite significant ARR growth, suggesting market consensus on category positioning matters more than individual growth rates.
- **Category position drives premium** - The fastest-growing company trades near the bottom multiple while slower growers command higher multiples, indicating investors value market leadership and category definition over raw growth acceleration.
- **Expect multiple expansion at scale** - Unlike traditional SaaS, AI harness multiples are expanding rather than compressing as companies scale, with recent acceleration to 100x+ multiples despite being 3x faster growth than 2021 benchmarks.
- **Track fundraising market conditions** - January 2026 showed acceleration of multiples for Legora, Sierra, and Ramp, suggesting favorable fundraising environments can drive valuation expansion independent of operational metrics.
