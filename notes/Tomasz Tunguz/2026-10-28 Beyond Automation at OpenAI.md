# Beyond Automation at OpenAI

**Source**: [Tomasz Tunguz](https://tomtunguz.com/arvind-kc-office-hours/)
**Author**: Tomasz Tunguz (featuring Arvind KC) | **Date**: Oct 28, 2026

---

## Summary

Arvind KC, OpenAI's Chief People Officer, argues that efficiency is the wrong frame for AI when model capabilities double every six months. He says the people function should amplify a company's most valuable capabilities, and that leadership shifts from managing a few people to directing many agents, with emotional intelligence becoming the leader's edge.

## Key Takeaways

- **Reframe AI goals beyond efficiency:** Stop asking only how to cut costs with AI, and ask which of your company's most valuable capabilities AI could amplify as capabilities keep compounding.
- **Democratize AI through existing strengths:** Instead of rebuilding the whole organization, identify the teams and skills that already create the most value and give them AI tools to multiply their impact.
- **Plan for a wider span of control:** Expect managers to direct many more agents alongside people, so design workflows, oversight, and review processes that can scale beyond seven direct reports.
- **Invest in emotional intelligence as a leadership skill:** As intelligence becomes cheap, prioritize hiring and developing leaders who excel at judgment, empathy, and communication, since these are harder to automate.
- **Treat the people function as a strategic lever:** Involve HR and people leaders in AI strategy so talent decisions, org design, and skill development keep pace with fast-changing model capabilities.
