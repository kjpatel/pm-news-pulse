# Sales in the Age of AI

**Author**: Tomasz Tunguz (featuring Bardia Shahali) | **Date**: Oct 12, 2026

## Summary

This Office Hours session announcement features Bardia Shahali, VP of Sales at Granola, discussing how sales organizations should be structured in the AI era. The core premise is that AI-era sales teams can run leaner, with faster ramps, larger deals, and go-to-market motions that spread on their own.

## Key Takeaways

- **Plan for smaller sales teams:** Model headcount with the expectation that AI-era teams can cover more revenue with fewer reps, and test whether your current territory and capacity assumptions still hold.
- **Re-benchmark quotas:** Stop using traditional SaaS quota benchmarks as the default, and set targets using your own ramp time, deal size, and product-led spread data.
- **Hire for larger deals and faster ramps:** Prioritize candidates who can handle bigger, more complex deals and who can become productive quickly, rather than optimizing for long-tenured SaaS experience.
- **Revisit rep compensation:** Adjust commission and comp plans to reflect larger deal sizes and shorter ramp periods, so incentives match the new sales motion.
- **Design GTM around product-led spread:** When the product spreads on its own, structure sales coverage to capture and expand existing adoption rather than building outbound motions from scratch.
