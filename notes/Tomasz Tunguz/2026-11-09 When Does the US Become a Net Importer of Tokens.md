# When Does the US Become a Net Importer of Tokens?

**Source**: [Tomasz Tunguz](https://tomtunguz.com/when-does-the-us-become-a-net-importer-of-tokens/)
**Author**: Tomasz Tunguz | **Date**: Nov 09, 2026

---

## Summary

The US could become a net importer of AI tokens between 2028 and 2030, because demand grows faster than the power grid and data center construction can absorb. Break-even is roughly 2.5x annual real token growth, and efficiency gains only offset demand up to that rate.

## Key Takeaways

- **Model the grid against token growth, not just chip count.** Power needed in gigawatts equals tokens demanded divided by tokens per watt, so track both demand growth and efficiency gains together when forecasting capacity.
- **Treat 2.5x annual token growth as the threshold to watch.** Below that rate, efficiency gains of about 2.9x a year can cover domestic demand; above it, imports become likely.
- **Stress-test infrastructure plans against cancellation rates.** The deadline moves from 2028 at 45% project failure to 2029 at 20%, so assume some planned facilities will not connect when setting capacity or sourcing plans.
- **Expect compute prices to rise under supply constraints rather than cut demand.** Short-term GPU rental prices jumped from $5 to over $13 per hour while demand barely slowed, so budget for premium compute costs.
- **Recognize capital cost as the dominant data center expense.** Capital recovery is about 64% of GPU-hour cost while power is only 5%, so a few points of cost of capital matter more than electricity rate differences.
