# What Does Pacing Mean?

**Source**: [Tomasz Tunguz](https://tomtunguz.com/what-does-the-pause-mean/)
**Author**: Tomasz Tunguz | **Date**: Sep 14, 2026

---

## Summary

Dario Amodei's call to 'pace' AI development lacks a concrete definition, with five competing camps (interpretability, labor, economic, geopolitical, and regulatory) each prioritizing different concerns but failing to specify an actual speed or mechanism for slowing innovation.

## Key Takeaways

- **Recognize the definitional gap**: 'Pacing' remains rhetorically appealing but practically undefined—no stakeholder has named a specific speed or timeline, making the concept difficult to operationalize or enforce.
- **Understand the five competing camps**: Interpretability researchers want time to understand models, labor advocates want worker protections, economists want growth for debt reduction, geopoliticians want competitive advantage over China, and regulatory capture skeptics resist new rules—each with different end goals.
- **Accept that compute thresholds fail**: The 2023 attempt to cap training at 10^26 FLOPS was revoked before any model hit it and became obsolete within weeks as training compute grows ~5x annually, making fixed numerical limits ineffective governance tools.
