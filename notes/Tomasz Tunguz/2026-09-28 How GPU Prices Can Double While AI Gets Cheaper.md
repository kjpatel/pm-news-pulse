# How GPU Prices Can Double While AI Gets Cheaper

**Source**: [Tomasz Tunguz](https://tomtunguz.com/how-gpu-prices-double-while-ai-gets-cheaper/)
**Author**: Tomasz Tunguz | **Date**: Sep 28, 2026

---

## Summary

GPU rental prices have doubled in six months while AI inference costs continue falling, a paradox resolved by rapidly improving efficiency gains that are outpacing hardware cost increases. The industry remains balanced between rising capital costs and exponential improvements in model efficiency.

## Key Takeaways

- **Track efficiency gains as the critical metric** - Monitor gross profit dollars per GPU-hour, not just raw GPU costs, since Claude improved 40% efficiency and OpenAI achieved 377x price reductions through margin innovation over 18 months.
- **Expect sustained GPU price pressure** - Capital deployment constraints, electricity bottlenecks (like Oracle's force majeure event), and rising interest rates will keep GPU costs elevated even as AI applications become cheaper for end users.
- **Validate the growth math with yield improvements** - Microsoft's 90% year-over-year token improvement per GPU suggests efficiency gains are outpacing cost increases, but this pace must be continuously verified to ensure the model remains sustainable.
