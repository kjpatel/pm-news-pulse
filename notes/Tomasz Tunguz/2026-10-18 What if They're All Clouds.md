# What if They're All Clouds?

**Source**: [Tomasz Tunguz](https://tomtunguz.com/what-if-they-are-all-clouds/)
**Author**: Tomasz Tunguz | **Date**: Oct 18, 2026

---

## Summary

Anthropic is usually treated as an AI company, but the article argues it should be valued as a cloud. On that basis, a $1.5T to $2T valuation would match Wall Street's implied value for a standalone Google Cloud (GCP) on about two-thirds of its revenue, with much faster growth.

## Key Takeaways

- **Value AI labs on infrastructure comparables.** Benchmark frontier model companies against cloud providers using revenue run rate, growth, and data center commitments rather than SaaS or pure-software multiples.
- **Track growth momentum, not just current scale.** Anthropic grew 15x year over year and is compounding about 4.9% a month, so exit run rate is a better guide than quarterly totals when projecting its position against AWS and Azure.
- **Watch customer concentration as a risk factor.** Two customers accounted for nearly 25% of Anthropic's 2025 revenue, so investors and operators should weigh that dependence against headline growth.
- **Look for the wedge that earns the platform label.** Amazon began with ecommerce, Google with search and ads, and Microsoft with the operating system; in AI, the equivalent wedge is a leading coding harness like Claude Code, which 87% of businesses buying coding agents on Ramp use.
- **Compare capacity commitments against run rate before judging ambition.** Anthropic's $518B in commitments is nearly 8x its Q3 run rate, while the hyperscalers committed roughly 1x to 2x theirs, so evaluate whether that capacity is funded by growth.
