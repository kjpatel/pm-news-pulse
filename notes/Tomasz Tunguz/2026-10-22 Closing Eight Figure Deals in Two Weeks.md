# Closing Eight Figure Deals in Two Weeks

**Source**: [Tomasz Tunguz](https://tomtunguz.com/closing-eight-figure-deals-in-two-weeks/)
**Author**: Tomasz Tunguz (featuring Bardia Shahali) | **Date**: Oct 22, 2026

---

## Summary

AI sales organizations differ sharply between application and infrastructure layers, with infrastructure deals closing in weeks at very large sizes amid intense competition. Dynamic markets require quarterly comp planning and enough staffing to keep outbound and expansion from being neglected.

## Key Takeaways

- **Set comp quarterly, not annually:** Model capabilities and competitive positions shift every few months, so annual compensation plans lag behind the market and should be reset each quarter.
- **Staff outbound and expansion deliberately:** Small teams tend to skip proactive outbound and expansion work, so reserve capacity for it rather than letting inbound and existing accounts absorb all effort.
- **Plan for complex infrastructure deal teams:** At the infrastructure layer, close eight-figure deals with AEs paired with forward-deployed engineers and sales engineers, and design the org and comp around that shared delivery model.
- **Expect faster cycles and higher quotas per rep:** In infrastructure sales, deals can reach tens of millions in about two weeks, and each rep can carry multiples of traditional quota, so size pipeline and targets accordingly.
- **Expect different motions at the application layer:** App-layer sales resemble traditional SaaS, with AEs and customer success managers driving adoption and expansion, though with higher productivity and typically faster adoption, so consider usage-based pricing and onboarding as core levers.
