# The Overhang

**Source**: [One Useful Thing](https://www.oneusefulthing.org/p/the-overhang)
**Author**: Ethan Mollick | **Date**: Sep 18, 2026

---

## Summary

AI is already capable of weeks of human-equivalent work when properly guided and harnessed, but most people underuse it. The gap between what models can do and what people actually do with them, the 'capability overhang,' is an opportunity for those who bring their own advantages: deep knowledge, wide knowledge, taste, and agency.

## Key Takeaways

- **Test what current models can actually do**: Give existing tools large, multi-step projects rather than small prompts, since many capabilities (such as operating 3D software or browsers) are not well understood until you try them.
- **Build deep expertise in your field**: Use your intuition to spot AI errors quickly and to see where it succeeds or fails, because specialists can judge the Jagged Frontier in their own domain.
- **Study widely across fields and traditions**: Learn the vocabulary and frameworks of design, reasoning, and other disciplines so you can ask the AI for specific changes, such as removing unnecessary 'eyebrow' headlines.
- **Exercise your own taste when making is cheap**: Since generating drafts and concepts is now fast, focus your effort on selecting, judging, and iterating on AI output, and push back when the first version is wrong.
- **Stay in the loop as the director**: Make the key decisions yourself, ask for revisions, and keep ownership of the outcome instead of delegating judgment wholesale to the AI.
