## Summary

Ibrahim Bashir announces a cohort-based workshop called The Asymptote Escape for product leaders who want to advance their careers. It will combine direct coaching with peer discussion on the challenges product leaders are facing now.

## Key Takeaways

- **Consider joining a peer cohort:** Product leaders who feel stuck can benefit from structured discussion with peers facing similar career and market pressures.
- **Bring a specific career question:** Since the format is direct coaching, come with a concrete situation, such as a promotion, role change, or strategy problem, to get the most from the sessions.
- **Use peer discussion to test your thinking:** Compare your approach to other leaders' experiences on current challenges before committing to a major career or product decision.
- **Watch the details before you commit:** The announcement is cut off before listing pricing, dates, and enrollment steps, so check those in the full post before signing up.
