# The Curation Problem

**Source**: [Run the Business](https://runthebusiness.substack.com/p/the-curation-problem)
**Author**: Ibrahim Bashir | **Date**: Sep 17, 2026

## Summary

Based on its subtitle, the article argues that AI makes it easier to produce content and ideas, which exposes a deficit in discovery: the ability to find, filter, and surface what actually matters. The full body text was not provided, so this summary is inferred from the title and subtitle.

## Key Takeaways

- **Audit your discovery process before adding more output:** Track how long it takes your team to find the right customer insight, prior research, or competitive signal, and fix the slowest step first.
- **Build a filter for AI-generated volume:** Define explicit criteria (relevance, evidence, novelty) that every AI-surfaced idea or content piece must meet before it reaches decision-makers.
- **Invest in curation roles and routines:** Assign an owner to regularly review, rank, and retire information sources, rather than assuming more tools will produce better discovery.
- **Separate generation from selection in your workflow:** Use AI to widen the search space, but reserve human judgment for choosing which options to pursue, and document why.
