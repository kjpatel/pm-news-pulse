# The Horizon Gap: Why GTM and Product Keep Missing Each Other

**Source**: [Run the Business](https://runthebusiness.substack.com/p/the-horizon-gap-why-gtm-and-product)
**Author**: Ibrahim Bashir | **Date**: Oct 27, 2026

---

## Summary

Product leaders who are newly co-owning revenue and retention targets with GTM teams often struggle to align their work with those outcomes. The article offers practical tips for bridging the gap between product and go-to-market so both teams can hit shared goals.

## Key Takeaways

- **Agree on shared revenue and retention metrics before roadmap debates.** Define which numbers product and GTM both own, and who is accountable for each, so prioritization starts from the same scoreboard.
- **Map the handoffs between product and GTM across the customer lifecycle.** Identify where activation, expansion, and churn move from marketing and sales into product, and where they move back, then assign owners to each handoff.
- **Build a regular review cadence that uses shared dashboards.** Hold a recurring meeting where product and GTM look at the same funnel and retention data together, rather than reporting on their own separate views.
- **Use a shared time horizon for planning.** Align product roadmap milestones with the sales cycle and renewal timing so that features land when GTM can actually use them to move revenue or retention.
- **Treat customer feedback from GTM as product input.** Set up a regular channel where account and sales teams surface churn reasons and deal blockers directly to product, and track whether those signals change what gets built.
