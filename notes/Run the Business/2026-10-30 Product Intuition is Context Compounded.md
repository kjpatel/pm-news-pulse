## Summary

The article's title frames product intuition as context compounded over time, but the body provided is only a short teaser for a video edition of a "5 minute Friday" post, so the core argument can't be confirmed from the text. The title suggests that strong product judgment comes from accumulating and applying context over time.

## Key Takeaways

- **Log product decisions and their outcomes** so you can review which signals actually predicted results and build a reusable record of context.
- **Revisit past calls on a regular cadence** to compare what you expected with what happened, sharpening your instincts with each iteration.
- **Deepen customer and market context continuously** through regular conversations, support tickets, and usage data rather than relying on occasional research sprints.
- **Share your reasoning with your team** by documenting the context behind decisions, so intuition becomes transferable rather than locked in one person's head.
