# Who Gets the Best Exec Roles?

**Source**: [Run the Business](https://runthebusiness.substack.com/p/who-gets-the-best-exec-roles)
**Author**: Ibrahim Bashir | **Date**: Sep 09, 2026

---

## Summary

The article argues that a strong network of career advocates, people who actively champion your work and advancement, is one of the most valuable assets an executive can have when pursuing top leadership roles. The provided text is only a title and subtitle, so this summary is based on that framing.

## Key Takeaways

- **Map your current advocates.** List the people who would speak up for you in a leadership discussion, and note where your network is thin, such as in other functions or senior levels.
- **Make your work visible to decision-makers.** Advocates can only champion you if they can describe your impact, so share concrete outcomes with the people who influence promotions and hiring.
- **Invest in relationships before you need them.** Stay in regular contact with former managers, peers, and mentors through useful exchanges rather than only when you are job hunting.
- **Give advocacy to get advocacy.** Actively recommend and promote others, which builds reciprocal goodwill and makes people more willing to vouch for you.
- **Equip your advocates with a clear story.** Give the people backing you a concise summary of your strengths, target roles, and the value you would bring so they can pitch you accurately.
