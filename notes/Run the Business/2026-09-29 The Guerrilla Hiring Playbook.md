# The Guerrilla Hiring Playbook

**Source**: [Run the Business](https://runthebusiness.substack.com/p/the-guerrilla-hiring-playbook)
**Author**: Ibrahim Bashir | **Date**: Sep 29, 2026

## Summary

The article argues that a company without the biggest brand can still win top talent by competing on compensation, offering higher pay bands and standing out in the hiring process. The piece is a short teaser, so its full argument isn't visible in the provided text.

## Key Takeaways

- **Benchmark your pay bands against the companies you lose candidates to.** Pull offer data from recent losses to find where your ranges fall short, then raise bands for the roles you struggle to fill.
- **Compete on the candidate's specific pain points rather than your brand.** Identify what top candidates want from a current employer, then lead outreach with how your role solves those problems.
- **Make your hiring process faster than the bigger company's.** Set a target of decisions within a few days of each interview stage, since speed can win candidates a brand-name employer would otherwise take.
- **Use aggressive, targeted sourcing to reach people before they apply.** Go after proven performers at competitors directly through referrals, referral bonuses, and personalized outreach.
