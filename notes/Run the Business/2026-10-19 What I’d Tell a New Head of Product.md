# What I’d Tell a New Head of Product

## Summary

The article frames a new Head of Product's core challenge as balancing two priorities: building the product itself and building the organization around it. The provided text is only a title and subtitle, so this summary is inferred from them rather than from the full piece.

## Key Takeaways

- **Decide where your time goes first.** Explicitly split your calendar between hands-on product decisions and team or org-building work, and revisit that split every quarter as the company's needs change.
- **Protect product quality while you hire and restructure.** Set a small set of product health metrics (e.g., activation, retention, customer feedback cadence) and review them weekly so org changes don't quietly degrade the product.
- **Hire for the gaps in the org, not just the roadmap.** Map which product functions (discovery, design, data, PM coverage) are missing or overloaded, and fill those first before adding headcount to existing teams.
- **Write down how product decisions get made.** Document decision rights, escalation paths, and review rhythms so the team can keep shipping without you in every meeting, which frees you to build the org.
- **Test your org design against product outcomes.** Every few months, check whether team boundaries and ownership still map to the customer journey, and reorganize when they don't.
