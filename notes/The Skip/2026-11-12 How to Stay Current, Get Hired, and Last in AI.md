# How to Stay Current, Get Hired, and Last in AI

**Source**: [The Skip](https://theskip.substack.com/p/how-to-stay-current-get-hired-and)
**Author**: Nikhyl Singhal (featuring Dana Ingraham, Hemal Shah, and Jiaona Zhang) | **Date**: Nov 12, 2026

---

## Summary

Three AI-forward PMs who recently took AI roles argue that much of the social media advice on staying current, getting hired, and avoiding burnout is dated, narrow, or wrong. They offer practical alternatives: rely on a small trusted network, learn in sustainable ways, hire for product fundamentals, and judge roles by the next job they enable.

## Key Takeaways

- **Build a small network you can call when things change.** Trust specific people for specific questions, such as which product to try, why a model is better, and whether other companies still use the big labs' models.
- **Pick one fixed test to run on every new model.** Using the same benchmark task gives you a stable point of comparison and something useful to offer the people you learn from.
- **Learn through work or existing time with family, not extra side projects.** Choose a setting where the job forces learning, or fold learning into time you already spend, so it lasts beyond a month.
- **Emphasize first-principles product thinking over tool fluency when interviewing or preparing.** Tools are easy to learn, while the ability to define the right problem is harder and is what hiring managers value.
- **Evaluate a role by the skills and next job it leads to, not by the company's valuation alone.** Ask whether customers love the product and whether you can learn to see what to build next, and consider forward-deployed or AI operations roles at AI-native companies.
