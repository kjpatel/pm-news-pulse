## Summary

The article examines how top executive recruiters evaluate candidates for head of product roles at the hottest companies, offering insight into where product management is heading. The provided text contains only the subtitle, so the full argument could not be verified.

## Key Takeaways

- **Map your experience to what top companies are currently hiring for:** Review recent head-of-product job descriptions from fast-growing companies and identify the skills they emphasize most, then close gaps in your own track record.
- **Build a clear narrative of product leadership impact:** Prepare two or three concrete examples of how you drove growth, shaped strategy, or led teams, since executive recruiters look for evidence of outcomes rather than titles.
- **Prepare for a role that may be expanding beyond traditional PM scope:** Assess whether your current experience covers AI-era responsibilities such as working with technical systems, pricing, and go-to-market decisions.
- **Network directly with executive recruiters:** Connect with recruiters who specialize in product leadership to get early visibility into openings and feedback on how the market is evaluating candidates.
