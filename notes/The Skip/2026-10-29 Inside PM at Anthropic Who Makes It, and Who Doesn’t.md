# Inside PM at Anthropic: Who Makes It, and Who Doesn’t

**Author**: Nikhyl Singhal | **Date**: Oct 29, 2026

## Summary

The article examines how Anthropic's research PM team hires, ramps new PMs, and retains them, with the goal of explaining who succeeds in the role and who doesn't. The available text is only a short description, so the full argument is not confirmed here.

## Key Takeaways

- **Define what the research PM role requires before you hire.** Write down the specific skills and working styles needed to partner with researchers, then screen candidates against those criteria rather than general PM experience.
- **Build a structured ramp plan for new PMs.** Set clear milestones for the first 30, 60, and 90 days, including how they learn the research domain and which stakeholders they must build trust with.
- **Give new hires early ownership of a bounded problem.** Early wins on a scoped project help a PM establish credibility with technical researchers and show whether they can operate in that environment.
- **Track retention drivers, not just hiring volume.** Review why people leave or stay, and use those signals to adjust onboarding, scope, and manager support.
- **Compare your own hiring bar against the traits that separate successful and struggling PMs.** Identify the two or three behaviors that predict success on your team and make them explicit in interviews and reviews.
