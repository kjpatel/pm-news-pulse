## Summary

The article describes a different kind of product job at Google's Gemini model team and argues that four habits from that role are worth borrowing. Only the subtitle was provided, so the specific habits are not available to confirm.

## Key Takeaways

- **Treat model product work as its own discipline:** Product management for a frontier model differs from conventional feature work, so define the role around model capabilities, evals, and release cadence rather than a standard roadmap.
- **Study the four habits before copying them:** The subtitle promises four habits worth borrowing, so capture each one with a concrete example from your own team before adopting it.
- **Pilot one habit for a month:** Pick the habit that best fits your current bottleneck, assign an owner, and measure whether decision speed or output quality improves before adding another.
- **Align product and model teams on shared metrics:** Agree on how success is measured for model-driven features so product decisions and model improvements move toward the same outcomes.
