# Congrats, your sales problems in PLG are completely unoriginal

**Source**: [Elena's Growth Scoop](https://www.elenaverna.com/p/congrats-your-sales-problems-in-plg)
**Author**: Elena Verna | **Date**: Sep 11, 2026

---

## Summary

PLG companies repeatedly face the same problems when scaling—from the tension between self-serve and enterprise motions to misaligned incentives around sales attribution—and recognizing these patterns as industry-wide rather than unique can help teams avoid costly mistakes.

## Key Takeaways

- **Preserve your PLG engine** while building enterprise: Don't abandon self-serve optimization when chasing big enterprise deals. Enterprise customers often start as small self-serve users who expand naturally; killing PLG momentum cuts off this pipeline.
- **Define leads accurately** using product behavior signals: Stop calling every signup a lead. Instead, identify genuine enterprise signals like team adoption, usage expansion, governance needs, and multi-department involvement before Sales engagement.
- **Align incentives to prevent internal competition**: Structure compensation and attribution to reward total revenue growth, not channel-specific metrics. When sales teams compete for credit over building value, you cannibalize revenue and damage customer relationships.
- **Let customers self-serve when they prefer it**: Avoid forcing users into sales conversations or moving accounts between revenue buckets for internal metrics. A self-serve customer who pays is a win, not a loss—trust the customer's buying preference.
