# Confessions of a recovering Full-Time Employee

**Source**: [Elena's Growth Scoop](https://www.elenaverna.com/p/confessions-of-a-recovering-full)
**Author**: Elena Verna (featuring Ibrahim) | **Date**: Nov 13, 2026

---

## Summary

Elena Verna and guest Ibrahim argue that many people are addicted to full-time employment because they undervalue solopreneurship and overvalue the stability of full-time roles. Ibrahim traces how his default of optimizing for income over expenses, and later for work that matters, led him to break free into a solo practice of side businesses and fractional gigs.

## Key Takeaways

- **Audit your career defaults.** Write down the rules you choose jobs by (take-home pay, prestige, stability) and check whether they still match what you want, since defaults set early tend to persist unexamined.
- **Compare the real margin, not just the salary.** Model total take-home after cost of living, taxes, and benefits, and weigh the opportunity cost of time spent in roles that pay well but don't build anything you value.
- **Test solopreneurship alongside your full-time job.** Build side projects or take a few fractional gigs to see how much income and fulfillment they can generate before deciding to leave a W-2 role.
- **Pick work that matters to you, not just work that scales your title.** Track whether your effort is building something you can point to with pride, and move away from roles where you give more to the company than you get back.
- **Be honest about what keeps you in full-time work.** Notice the pull of the paycheck and the corporate ladder, and set a specific trigger, such as a savings runway or a fixed number of fractional clients, that would justify making the switch.
