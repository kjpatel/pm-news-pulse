# Here is what women want from AI.

**Source**: [Elena's Growth Scoop](https://www.elenaverna.com/p/dear-new-york-times-thats-not-what)
**Author**: Elena Verna | **Date**: Oct 27, 2026

---

## Summary

The author argues that the NYT framing of 'what women want from AI' as a question about shopping, childcare, and household management is too narrow, and that AI should instead expand women's access to building companies, capital, careers, and influence. She contends that how the conversation is framed shapes who sees AI as meant for them.

## Key Takeaways

- **Audit how your product and marketing frame users.** Check whether you default to consumer or household use cases for a group and ask whether that framing limits who sees your product as built for them.
- **Design for builders, not just consumers.** Test whether your AI features help users create, earn, or lead, not only manage tasks, and prioritize at least one capability that lets users build something of their own.
- **Address the funding gap in how you think about markets.** Recognize that female-founded companies raised 1.1% of US VC in 2025 ($3.2B vs. $191.1B), and consider how investor and founder assumptions shape which opportunities get backed.
- **Create open, low-barrier building programs.** Run hackathons or onboarding paths that help people without engineering backgrounds turn ideas into working products, as the Lovable SheBuilds program does.
- **Question who carries invisible labor in your target segments.** Ask whether your AI removes the household or organizational burden for everyone or simply makes one group more efficient at it, and whether the other partner or team member is expected to share the work.
