## Summary

Ben Thompson interviews analyst Craig Moffett about SpaceX's trajectory across launch, broadband, and mobile, and how he applies lessons from the dot-com bubble to today's AI and space-company valuations. The article is paywalled, so this summary is based on the publicly visible teaser.

## Key Takeaways

- **Test valuations against the dot-com playbook:** Compare today's high-growth AI and space valuations with late-1990s patterns, such as expectations that outrun unit economics and capital raised ahead of proven revenue.
- **Evaluate SpaceX as a multi-segment business:** Model launch, broadband, and mobile separately, since each has different capital intensity, competitive dynamics, and timelines to profitability.
- **Track the capital-intensity question:** Monitor how much spending each SpaceX segment requires and whether the financing assumptions behind it hold up as the company scales.
- **Use Moffett's bubble lens for your own bets:** Before committing to a sector narrative, write down the specific metrics that would show whether the enthusiasm is justified or has become a bubble.
