# Do You Trust Apple?

**Source**: [Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/2026/do-you-trust-apple/)
**Author**: Ben Thompson | **Date**: Oct 19, 2026

---

## Summary

Ben Thompson argues that Apple's forthcoming HomeView smart-home product is less interesting than the prospect of owning the software layer yourself, since AI makes it feasible to decompile existing software and build custom tools that work with your own agent. He is skeptical that Apple's constraints and reliance on third-party integrations will deliver a good smart-home experience.

## Key Takeaways

- **Question platform constraints before adopting a new device category.** Evaluate whether a product's integrations depend on third-party developers who may ship poor software, and whether the vendor's limits will reach parts of your life you would rather keep open.
- **Prioritize control of the software layer over hardware convenience.** Favor setups where you can direct the software that interacts with your agent, rather than waiting for a vendor to expose the right API.
- **Use AI to adapt or rebuild software you need.** Decompiling and porting existing software is becoming practical, so consider building custom tools for recurring needs instead of accepting a closed ecosystem's default experience.
- **Judge marketing demos skeptically.** Highly produced keynote videos can obscure whether a product's use cases make practical sense, so test the stated use cases against your real routines before buying in.
- **Audit your reliance on any single ecosystem.** Periodically assess how deeply your devices, data, and workflows are tied to one vendor, and identify where switching or self-hosting would be feasible.
