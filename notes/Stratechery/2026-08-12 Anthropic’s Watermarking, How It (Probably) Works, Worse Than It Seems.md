# Anthropic’s Watermarking, How It (Probably) Works, Worse Than It Seems

## Summary

Anthropic's implementation of watermarking in response to EU AI law is fundamentally flawed, creating problems that are worse than the issues it attempts to solve due to philosophical and practical concerns about AI output verification and regulation.

## Key Takeaways

- **Understand watermarking limitations** - AI watermarking cannot reliably distinguish between AI-generated and human-created content, making regulatory compliance based on watermarks inherently ineffective.
- **Recognize regulatory unintended consequences** - EU AI laws mandating watermarking may push compliance-averse companies toward less transparent solutions rather than improving AI safety and accountability.
- **Evaluate compliance costs** - Implementing watermarking systems creates operational overhead for AI companies while failing to achieve the stated goal of content authenticity verification.
