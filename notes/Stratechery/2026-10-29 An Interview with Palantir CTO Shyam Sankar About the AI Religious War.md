# An Interview with Palantir CTO Shyam Sankar About the AI Religious War

**Source**: [Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/2026/an-interview-with-palantir-cto-shyam-sankar-about-the-ai-religious-war/)
**Author**: Ben Thompson (featuring Shyam Sankar) | **Date**: Oct 29, 2026

---

## Summary

Palantir CTO Shyam Sankar argues that the AI industry underinvests in diffusion, that companies focused on top-line growth adopt AI more successfully, and that labs should take a systems engineering approach to safety. He also frames the debate as a broader 'religious war' over technology's role in society.

## Key Takeaways

- **Prioritize diffusion over model research:** Shift AI investment toward getting existing capabilities into real workflows, since adoption, not just frontier capability, drives value.
- **Start AI projects from top-line goals:** Tie AI initiatives to revenue growth and customer outcomes, because Sankar suggests companies focused on the top line adopt AI more successfully than those focused on cost cuts.
- **Apply systems engineering to AI safety:** Evaluate safety as an end-to-end system with deployment context, failure modes, and controls, rather than relying only on model-level guardrails.
- **Keep production capacity close to innovation:** Avoid offshoring the production work that generates the feedback and motivation for innovation, since Sankar argues that innovation follows from productivity.
- **Use forward-deployed engineers to embed in customer problems:** Place engineers directly with customers to learn their real operational constraints and turn those lessons into product improvements.
