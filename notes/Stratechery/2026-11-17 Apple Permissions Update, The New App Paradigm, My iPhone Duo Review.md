## Key Takeaways

- **Evaluate agent support, not just app features:** When assessing an Apple or other platform update, check whether it lets AI agents act across apps or only improves individual apps.
- **Question the app-centric model:** Treat the app as one possible unit of computing rather than the default, and note where agent-driven workflows could bypass app boundaries.
- **Read the Mac and iPhone updates separately:** Judge each platform on its own merits, since the Mac may be improving while the agent gap remains open.
- **Use the iPhone Duo review as a hands-on reference:** Before buying or recommending the device, read the review for concrete usage observations rather than relying on launch marketing.
- **Watch for permissions as an agent constraint:** Track how OS permission models shape what agents can access, because these rules will determine which agent use cases are practical.
