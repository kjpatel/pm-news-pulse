# The End of the Internet

**Source**: [Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/2026/the-end-of-the-internet/)
**Author**: Ben Thompson | **Date**: Oct 26, 2026

---

## Summary

Ben Thompson traces the Internet's evolution from abundant blogs to ad-driven search and engagement-driven social media, arguing that each wave's open access created infinite competition that eroded its business value. He contends that the sustainable response is to go small, through paid subscriptions to niche audiences and high-trust group chats.

## Key Takeaways

- **Serve a niche rather than chase scale.** When distribution is free and content is abundant, limit your audience to people who truly value your work and monetize them directly.
- **Build a paid subscription model around a distinct voice.** Writing with low substitutability, like Andrew Sullivan's, lets readers pay for something they can't get elsewhere.
- **Invest in discovery, not just distribution.** Because distribution is free, the scarce resource is helping the right people find you, so focus on curation and search visibility.
- **Shift community efforts toward private group spaces.** Public social platforms tend to become engagement-tuned entertainment, so concentrate relationship-building in smaller, high-trust groups.
- **Treat openness as a competitive risk, not only an opportunity.** Ask whether a low-barrier market will lead to infinite competition that erodes margins, and plan defenses accordingly.
