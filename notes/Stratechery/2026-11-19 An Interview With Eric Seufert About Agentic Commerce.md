# An Interview With Eric Seufert About Agentic Commerce

**Source**: [Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/2026/an-interview-with-eric-seufert-about-agentic-commerce/)
**Author**: Ben Thompson (featuring Eric Seufert) | **Date**: Nov 19, 2026

---

## Summary

Ben Thompson interviews Eric Seufert about agentic commerce, where AI agents make purchasing decisions on users' behalf. Seufert argues that advertising will be the business model that makes agentic commerce work, and Thompson argues that retailers should design their storefronts to serve agents.

## Key Takeaways

- **Treat AI agents as a distinct storefront audience.** Retailers should evaluate whether their product data, checkout flows, and catalogs can be read and acted on by agents, not only by human shoppers.
- **Plan for ads as the monetization layer for agent-mediated buying.** Seufert's view that advertising will be the answer suggests businesses should model how sponsored placement or agent-facing promotion would affect margins before committing to a commerce strategy.
- **Make your catalog machine-legible now.** Structured, accurate product attributes, pricing, and availability are what agents rely on to compare options, so auditing and cleaning this data is an immediate, concrete step.
- **Prioritize agent-friendly retailers when choosing partners.** Thompson's preference for retailers who cater to agents suggests vetting which sales channels and marketplaces will support agent transactions well before investing heavily in them.
