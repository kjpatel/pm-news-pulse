# 2026.43: A New Era

**Source**: [Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/2026/a-new-era/)
**Author**: Ben Thompson | **Date**: Oct 23, 2026

---

## Summary

Ben Thompson announces that Stripe is now Stratechery's presenting sponsor, the site's first advertising, and explains why he sees subscriptions, which Stripe's recurring billing made viable for him, as the better model than ads. The post also rounds up the week's coverage, including the iPhone Duo, Netflix's troubles, and a Nathan Fielder documentary.

## Key Takeaways

- **Evaluate sponsorship conflicts openly.** Thompson argues that a paying sponsor makes objectivity hard, so he discloses the relationship up front and reasons through his own bias before readers have to.
- **Build recurring revenue around direct customers.** Thompson chose subscriptions over ads because a one-person site can't rely on advertising, and recurring billing made that model workable. Consider whether a subscription or membership model fits your own publication or product.
- **Use multiple formats to reach different audiences.** Stratechery is adding in-person, on-video Sharp Tech episodes and a weekly Sunday bonus episode for subscribers, which extends the value of existing content.
- **Separate fixable problems from structural ones when assessing a company.** Andrew Sharp splits Netflix's troubles into content issues that can be fixed and the harder challenge of growing a business against Meta and YouTube for engagement.
- **Weigh product trade-offs in hardware reviews.** The iPhone Duo review shows that a device can excel at one job (an iPad that fits in your pocket) while falling short at another (phone functionality). Decide which use case matters most before buying.
