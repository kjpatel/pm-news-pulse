## Summary

As AI infrastructure buildout accelerates, capital constraints are emerging as a critical limiting factor alongside compute and power, with Nvidia implementing creative financing mechanisms to bridge the funding gap while risking a bubble expansion.

## Key Takeaways

- **Monitor capital availability** as the third constraint in AI infrastructure alongside compute and power; when companies can't fund buildout despite clear ROI potential, financing innovation becomes as important as hardware innovation.
- **Watch for financial engineering risks** like Nvidia's new funding mechanisms that tap long-duration capital—these expand the blast radius of potential bubbles while subsidizing threatened margins across the AI ecosystem.
- **Recognize the sustainability question** in AI economics: strong value propositions haven't yet translated into self-sustaining revenue, making external capital and creative financing essential bridges rather than optional tools.
