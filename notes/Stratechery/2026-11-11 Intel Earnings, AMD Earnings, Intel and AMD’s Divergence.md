## Summary

## Key Takeaways

- **Compare chipmakers through their history, not just quarterly numbers.** Earnings divergence between Intel and AMD usually traces back to structural, long-run differences in how each company was built and operates.
- **Assess whether a company's current strategy has drifted from its founding strengths.** Intel's struggles may stem from abandoning an earlier path, so check whether recent decisions still match what made the company successful.
- **Treat "returning to an old path" as a strategic option to evaluate.** Before dismissing a legacy approach, identify which of its original principles still apply to current market conditions.
- **Read earnings alongside competitive positioning.** Compare the strategic choices behind two companies' results, since the reasons for divergence often matter more than the headline figures.
