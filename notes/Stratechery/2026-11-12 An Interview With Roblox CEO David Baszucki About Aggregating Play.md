# An Interview With Roblox CEO David Baszucki About Aggregating Play

**Source**: [Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/2026/an-interview-with-roblox-ceo-david-baszucki-about-aggregating-play/)
**Author**: Ben Thompson (featuring David Baszucki) | **Date**: Nov 12, 2026

---

## Summary

Ben Thompson interviews Roblox CEO David Baszucki about how AI is making it possible for anyone to create games, which lets Roblox pursue an Aggregator strategy built on discovery, recommendation, and a universal player. The conversation covers the shift from coding harnesses to game creation harnesses, the platform's advertising model, and whether Roblox still sees itself as building the metaverse.

## Key Takeaways

- **Treat AI as a paintbrush accelerator, not a replacement:** Baszucki says the data shows AI augmenting human creators, so keep human ideas and novel gameplay central while using AI to speed up execution.
- **Build for the creation harness, not just the coding harness:** Game creation spans art, 3D assets, scenes, avatars, missions, and code, so product teams should evaluate whether their AI tools handle multiple modalities and listen to creative intent.
- **Track AI-contribution metrics as leading indicators:** Roblox measures MCP plug-ins from tools like Claude Code and Codex, the count of AI-touched assets, and AI-built games in the top charts, giving a concrete way to measure adoption of AI-assisted creation.
- **Lower the barrier to creation to grow the supply side:** Roblox's goal is for making games to be as accessible as playing them, so reduce friction for non-engineers and non-PMs to build inside your product.
- **Use recommendation as the core of an aggregator strategy:** When supply becomes abundant, discovery and recommendation determine value, so invest in ranking and matching systems before expanding supply further.
