# Muse Spark Open, Meta + Apple, Thin and Thick

**Author**: Ben Thompson | **Date**: Oct 20, 2026

## Summary

Meta has released a new open-source model, Muse Spark Open, and is partnering with Apple so users can run it locally on their devices. The full analysis is behind Stratechery Plus, so this summary is based only on the publicly visible teaser.

## Key Takeaways

- **Test local deployment of open models on Apple hardware:** If you evaluate open models, check whether Muse Spark Open runs acceptably on your Apple devices to gauge the practical value of on-device inference.
- **Weigh privacy and cost benefits of local inference:** Consider which of your AI workloads could run locally rather than through a paid cloud API, since local models can reduce data exposure and per-token spend.
- **Track the Meta-Apple partnership as a platform signal:** Watch whether Apple's support for third-party open models expands, as it could change which models developers build against.
- **Reassess your vendor dependence on frontier models:** Compare Muse Spark Open's capabilities against your current hosted model for your core tasks before committing to long-term contracts.
