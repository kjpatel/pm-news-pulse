# Presented by Stripe

**Source**: [Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/2026/presented-by-stripe/)
**Author**: Ben Thompson | **Date**: Oct 21, 2026

---

## Summary

Stratechery is now presented by Stripe, an exclusive sponsorship that Ben Thompson argues makes his long-informal conflicts of interest explicit while preserving editorial independence. He says the revenue will fund better products, starting with a video version of Sharp Tech.

## Key Takeaways

- **Disclose conflicts formally instead of hiding them:** Thompson argues that informal ties to Stripe were already shaping his coverage, so he now discloses the relationship in every relevant post rather than quietly writing about it less.
- **Test sponsor deals against written editorial terms:** Thompson says his contract guarantees editorial independence and that Stripe cannot dictate what he writes or omits, so readers and creators negotiating sponsorships should secure similar explicit clauses.
- **Reinvest sponsorship revenue into the product:** The deal funds a home video studio and in-person recording of Sharp Tech, showing how ad or sponsor income can directly improve what subscribers receive.
- **Keep subscription prices stable despite new ads:** Thompson does not anticipate raising subscription prices during the deal, a reminder that paying subscribers should be treated as the primary relationship even when sponsors are added.
- **Make sponsor messaging something you actually believe:** Thompson says he will only run ads he truly endorses, so sponsors should be chosen for authentic fit with the audience rather than purely for revenue.
