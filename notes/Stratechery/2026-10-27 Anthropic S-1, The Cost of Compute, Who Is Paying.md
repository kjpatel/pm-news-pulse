# Anthropic S-1, The Cost of Compute, Who Is Paying?

**Source**: [Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/2026/anthropic-s-1-the-cost-of-compute-who-is-paying/)
**Author**: Ben Thompson | **Date**: Oct 27, 2026

---

## Summary

Anthropic's IPO filing shows a very profitable core business, but Ben Thompson argues that competition may eat all of those profits. Investors also face endless dilution as the company raises capital to fund compute.

## Key Takeaways

- **Separate core-business profitability from durable profit.** Before treating a high-margin AI lab's margins as sustainable, model how much competitors could erode them over the next few years.
- **Stress-test dilution when evaluating AI IPOs.** Estimate the future share issuance needed to fund compute, and compare it with projected per-share returns before pricing in growth.
- **Track who is paying for compute at each layer of the stack.** Map which party bears the cost of inference and training, since that determines where margins can actually accrue.
- **Benchmark AI company economics against competitors' unit costs.** Compare revenue per unit of compute and gross margin trends across providers to spot which business is at risk of being commoditized.
