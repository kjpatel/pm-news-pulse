## Summary

Airlines are using AI to dynamically price tickets in real-time, shrinking opportunities for finding bargain fares. Hunter Walk recommends using Junova, an AI-powered tool that automatically claims flight credits when ticket prices drop below what you paid.

## Key Takeaways

- **Use price-tracking tools** like Junova to automatically monitor booked flights and claim credits when prices drop, with a fair commission structure (25% of savings) rather than upfront fees.
- **Understand airline pricing dynamics** - AI is enabling carriers to adjust seat prices continuously based on dozens of variables, making it harder to find deals through traditional shopping methods.
- **Leverage referral programs** to offset costs further, as Hunter's referral link provides $25 credits to both parties when price drops are claimed.
