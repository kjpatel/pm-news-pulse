# Focus on Finding Role Models, Not Mentors

**Source**: [Hunter Walk](https://hunterwalk.com/2026/11/06/focus-on-finding-role-models-not-mentors/)
**Author**: Hunter Walk | **Date**: Nov 06, 2026

---

## Summary

Hunter Walk argues that the term 'mentor' has been diluted into social signaling, so he prefers 'role models': people who show a useful style, path, or framework in at least one area of life. He notes role models need not be perfect or know their influence, and that being a role model scales far better than mentoring.

## Key Takeaways

- **Look for role models in specific facets of life.** Choose people for one admirable trait, path, or framework, such as how they built their career, rather than requiring them to be exemplary in every way.
- **Study someone from afar before emulating them.** Research their work and choices so you understand what actually appeals to you and avoid misreading their approach.
- **Tell role models how they've helped you.** Briefly and sincerely acknowledging their positive impact is worthwhile, even though they may not have realized they were serving that purpose.
- **Be a role model by sharing consistently and listening.** Put enough of your work and perspective into the world on a regular basis, then let engaged followers' questions draw out more openness rather than repeating one story.
- **Stop claiming mentorship over others.** Avoid unilaterally labeling yourself someone's mentor, since that recognition should come from the other person.
