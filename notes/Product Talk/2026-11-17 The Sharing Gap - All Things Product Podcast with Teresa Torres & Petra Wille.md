# The Sharing Gap - All Things Product Podcast with Teresa Torres & Petra Wille

**Source**: [Product Talk](https://www.producttalk.org/the-sharing-gap-all-things-product-podcast-with-teresa-torres-petra-wille/)
**Author**: Teresa Torres (featuring Petra Wille) | **Date**: Nov 17, 2026

---

## Summary

The useful, practical side of product work, the how, the messy middle, and the small wins, is disappearing from social media as platitudes and AI slop crowd it out. Product leaders hold back real work because they assume others are further ahead, and the authors argue that sharing specific, personal experience is both valuable and low-risk.

## Key Takeaways

- **Share unglamorous results too.** Even a result like "we tried 20 things and saw no efficiency gains" is a learning others need, so don't wait for a polished win before posting.
- **Write only about what you personally do.** Teresa stopped writing generic guides and focused on her own work, such as building Claude Code workflows and a year of AI evals, and her posts performed better afterward.
- **Start with a 5-day challenge.** Each day, pick the most interesting thing that happened in your work and post it. Most posts will be ignored, which keeps the risk low while you build the habit.
- **Write for the person one step behind you.** Describe what you faced, what you did, and what you hope happens, and skip the ending, since there is always someone who can benefit from your current stage.
- **Treat the reluctance to share as product-level imposter syndrome.** Teams assume a competitor does it better and that their own story is too small or too niche, but being too close to your own work hides what is interesting about it.
