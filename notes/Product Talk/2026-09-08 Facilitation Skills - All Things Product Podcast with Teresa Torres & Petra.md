# Facilitation Skills - All Things Product Podcast with Teresa Torres & Petra Wille

**Source**: [Product Talk](https://www.producttalk.org/facilitation-skills-all-things-product-with-teresa-torres-petra-wille/)
**Author**: Teresa Torres (featuring Petra Wille) | **Date**: Sep 08, 2026

---

## Summary

Conflict on a product team is not inherently a warning sign; the absence of disagreement often is. Teresa Torres and Petra Wille argue that teams should distinguish task conflict from relationship conflict, since each needs a different response, and that facilitation is a learnable skill product people should build rather than outsource.

## Key Takeaways

- **Protect time for retrospectives.** Regularly pause to ask "how are we working together?" and treat it as a non-negotiable team practice, not an agile ritual to drop.
- **Write a team charter covering working norms.** Document working hours, communication preferences, and how each person likes to make decisions, so implicit norms become explicit before friction builds.
- **Sort conflicts before responding to them.** Label disagreements as task conflict (about the work) or relationship conflict (about how you work together), then address task conflict through shared discovery and relationship conflict through charters and direct one-on-one conversations.
- **Turn opinion battles into experiments.** When two people disagree on an approach, design a quick test to settle it instead of escalating the debate.
- **Choose your facilitator deliberately.** Pick someone whose context helps rather than hurts neutrality; an embedded coach may know too much history, so a product person or senior engineer with strong facilitation instincts may be the better choice.
