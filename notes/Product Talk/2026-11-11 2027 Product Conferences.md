# 2027 Product Conferences

**Source**: [Product Talk](https://www.producttalk.org/2027-product-conferences/)
**Author**: Teresa Torres | **Date**: Nov 11, 2026

---

## Summary

Teresa Torres publishes a curated list of 2027 product conferences, organized by month with dates, names, and locations. It invites readers to suggest missing events and notes the list will be updated as more events are announced.

## Key Takeaways

- **Plan your 2027 conference calendar early:** Scan the monthly listings now and shortlist events in your region or budget before spring registration windows fill up.
- **Prioritize events that match your career stage:** Pick a mix of general product conferences (such as ProductCon or Product-Led Summit) and niche ones (such as AI Product Summit or Chief Product Officer Summit) based on whether you need broad learning or specialized peers.
- **Consider adjacent disciplines for cross-functional skills:** Growth, UX, customer success, and sales enablement summits can help product managers build the go-to-market and design fluency that increasingly shapes product decisions.
- **Suggest missing events to the list:** If you know a relevant conference that isn't listed, email conferences@producttalk.org so the community benefits and the list stays current.
- **Check back for updates:** Since the list will grow over time, revisit it periodically, especially the empty November and December sections, to catch newly announced events.
