# Unpacking Innovation - All Things Product Podcast with Teresa Torres & Petra Wille

**Source**: [Product Talk](https://www.producttalk.org/unpacking-innovation-all-things-product-podcast-with-teresa-torres-petra-wille/)
**Author**: Teresa Torres (featuring Petra Wille) | **Date**: Sep 29, 2026

---

## Summary

Teresa Torres and Petra Wille argue that innovation is a means to an end, not a goal, and that it matters only when it solves a customer problem better. Using the history of the login as an example, they show how novelty can add complexity and cognitive cost for users, while simplicity and reduction can themselves be innovative.

## Key Takeaways

- **Treat innovation as a means, not a goal.** Before pursuing a novel approach, ask whether it serves the customer better than a familiar solution.
- **Accept familiar patterns when they are the best fit.** Optimizing for the best solution sometimes lands on an existing pattern, and that is fine rather than a failure to be novel.
- **Count the cognitive cost of novelty.** Every new flow asks users to learn a new system, so weigh that learning burden against the benefit before shipping it.
- **Look for innovation through reduction.** Removing steps, leaving things out, and minimizing friction can be a legitimate form of innovation on the user's behalf.
- **Run cross-functional discovery to find 'just now possible' moments.** Bring engineers into customer problem discovery so they can spot when new technology makes a real struggle easier to solve.
