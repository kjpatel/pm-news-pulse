# Delivery Isn’t Free - All Things Product Podcast with Teresa Torres & Petra Wille

**Source**: [Product Talk](https://www.producttalk.org/delivery-isnt-free-all-things-product-podcast-with-teresa-torres-petra-wille/)
**Author**: Teresa Torres (featuring Petra Wille) | **Date**: Sep 15, 2026

---

## Summary

AI has made building a single feature dramatically cheaper, but delivering a real, production-quality product has not gotten cheaper, so 'delivery is free' is a false claim. Teams that treat coding agents as free end up with messy code, bloated features, and AI products that take months or years to move from a convincing prototype to a trustworthy product.

## Key Takeaways

- **Separate throwaway prototypes from production code.** Use cheap, AI-generated prototypes for discovery, but actually throw them away instead of promoting them to production.
- **Keep engineers steering architecture.** Have a skilled engineering team review what agents produce and make data model and architecture decisions, rather than outsourcing them to a coding agent.
- **Watch for the 'feature 15' problem.** Periodically audit your data model and code for duplication and performance regressions, since AI-accelerated feature bloat compounds into a Frankenstein structure.
- **Budget for the last 30%.** Treat getting to a good-looking prototype (roughly 60-70%) as the fast part, and plan months of work for error analysis, evals, and iteration to reach a trustworthy AI product.
- **Use domain experts to review AI output.** Have people with deep domain knowledge check AI-generated artifacts, such as opportunity solution trees, for quality issues that generic review will miss.
