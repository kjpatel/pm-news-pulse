# My Personal Annual Review Process (Now a Claude Code Plugin)

**Source**: [Product Talk](https://www.producttalk.org/personal-annual-review/)
**Author**: Teresa Torres | **Date**: Nov 18, 2026

---

## Summary

Teresa Torres shares her structured December personal annual review process, which reflects on life categories, lists 100 wishes, and sets outcome and process goals, and she packages it as a Claude Code plugin for subscribers. She argues the value lies in the reflection and deliberate planning, not in hitting the plan exactly.

## Key Takeaways

- **Reserve time each December for a structured annual review.** Block several weeks to reflect on the past year and plan the next, letting the exercises simmer rather than rushing them.
- **Write 100 wishes every year.** Forcing yourself to list 100 wishes across categories reveals what you truly want, and comparing lists over years shows which desires recur and which are fleeting.
- **Turn outcome goals into process goals with clear targets.** For each goal, define the outcome you want, the concrete habits that lead to it, and how you will track progress.
- **Choose the life categories you want to review before starting.** Pick the areas that matter most to you, such as work, health, relationships, finances, or hobbies, and run the same four exercises for each.
- **Use an AI thought partner to spot patterns you might miss.** Ask Claude to surface themes in your reflections and to push vague goals toward specific, trackable process goals.
