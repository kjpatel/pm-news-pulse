# Ch. 11: Measuring Impact

**Source**: [Product Talk](https://www.producttalk.org/ch-11-measuring-impact/)
**Author**: Teresa Torres | **Date**: Nov 16, 2026

---

## Summary

Teresa Torres argues that product teams must go beyond testing assumptions and measure whether their changes actually move the desired business outcome. Teams should start with small, targeted instrumentation, and measuring hard-to-track outcomes is worth pursuing even when the impact happens off-platform.

## Key Takeaways

- **Instrument only what you need**: For each assumption test, define the specific metric, the trigger that starts measurement, and the evaluation criteria before building anything. Avoid trying to measure everything.
- **Measure the outcome, not just the test**: Track whether addressing an opportunity moves your product and business outcome, not only whether the assumption test succeeded.
- **Test the link between product and business outcomes**: Identify the assumed connection between your product outcome and your business outcome, then design a test to validate it.
- **Tackle your hardest-to-measure outcome**: Pick the part of your desired outcome that is hardest to measure, identify why it's hard, and start with an imperfect proxy plus a small experiment to begin collecting data.
- **Start small and iterate**: Begin with a rough measurement, as AfterCollege did when it grew its response rate from 5% to 37% over time, rather than waiting for a perfect instrumentation plan.
