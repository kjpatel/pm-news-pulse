# Discovery & The 4 Risks - All Things Product Podcast with Teresa Torres & Petra Wille

**Author**: Teresa Torres (featuring Petra Wille) | **Date**: Nov 10, 2026

---

## Summary

Teresa Torres and Petra Wille apply product thinking to continuous discovery itself, asking whether the habits have product-market fit with the organizations that buy training. Teresa concludes the habits work well for individuals and teams but struggle to stick at the organizational level, so the solution may need to fit existing constraints rather than demand that organizations change.

## Key Takeaways

- **Diagnose where discovery breaks:** Check whether the failure is willingness (protecting time, customer access) or organizational structure, since the habits usually hold at the team level and fail at the org level.
- **Work within quarterly incentives:** Treat boards' demand for predictable results and executives' pressure for certainty as fixed, then adapt your discovery approach to fit those constraints.
- **Secure a long-term committed leader:** Lasting adoption of discovery depends on a sponsor who stays a long time, so plan for leadership changes and build support beyond one champion.
- **Start with a single team:** Partial adoption is valuable, because once a team has worked this way, what has been seen cannot be unseen, and that experience creates momentum for wider change.
- **Test your solution, not just the organization:** When adoption stalls, question whether your approach is wrong before concluding the organization is the problem.
