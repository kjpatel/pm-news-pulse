# Trash Can Tracking - All Things Product Podcast with Teresa Torres & Petra Wille

**Source**: [Product Talk](https://www.producttalk.org/trash-can-tracking-all-things-product-podcast-with-teresa-torres-petra-wille/)
**Author**: Teresa Torres (featuring Petra Wille) | **Date**: Sep 22, 2026

---

## Summary

Petra Wille and Teresa Torres argue that tracking discarded work with trash can symbols on discovery and delivery boards makes a team's decision-making visible. The contents of the trash cans, especially empty ones, reveal how healthy a team's innovation, strategy, and discovery culture really is.

## Key Takeaways

- **Add trash can symbols to both your discovery and delivery boards** so that work you deliberately abandon is tracked as carefully as work you ship.
- **Treat an empty solution-space trash can as a red flag**, since it usually means the team is not comparing multiple options before committing.
- **Read an empty problem-space trash can in context** by checking whether your strategy and vision are strong enough to justify the filtering, rather than assuming innovation is lacking.
- **Use the trash can as a ritual to retire zombie opportunities** that the organization keeps resurfacing after the team has already decided to walk away from them.
- **Audit whether problems can reach your board at all** by asking if support agents, salespeople, engineers, and customers feel safe surfacing them, since psychological safety shapes which problems get assessed.
