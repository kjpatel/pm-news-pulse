# Ch. 10: Testing Assumptions

**Source**: [Product Talk](https://www.producttalk.org/ch-10-testing-assumptions/)
**Author**: Teresa Torres | **Date**: Sep 21, 2026

---

## Summary

Teresa Torres argues that teams should test assumptions across multiple ideas rather than one idea at a time, using simulated experiences and behavioral observation. Success criteria must be defined upfront so results are unambiguous, and small early signals are preferable to large experiments.

## Key Takeaways

- **Define success criteria before running the test:** Write down the exact threshold for passing or failing (e.g., "7 out of 10 people complete the task") so that someone else could call the result without debate.
- **Test assumptions across several ideas at once:** Rather than testing one whole idea at a time, identify the riskiest assumptions across a set of solutions to reduce confirmation bias and escalation of commitment.
- **Simulate an experience and observe behavior:** Design tests that put people in a realistic moment and watch what they actually do, rather than asking customers what they would do in the future.
- **Start with the smallest test you can run in 1-2 days:** Choose one riskiest assumption, define the moment, behavior, sample size, and pass threshold, and shrink the test if it cannot be completed that quickly.
- **Audit your team's past tests:** Review 3-5 recent experiments to check whether success criteria were defined upfront and specific, then pick one practice to change going forward.
