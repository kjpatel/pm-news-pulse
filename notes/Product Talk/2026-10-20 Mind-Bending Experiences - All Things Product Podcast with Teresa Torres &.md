# Mind-Bending Experiences - All Things Product Podcast with Teresa Torres & Petra Wille

**Author**: Teresa Torres (featuring Petra Wille) | **Date**: Oct 20, 2026

---

## Summary

Team experiences that are strange, uncomfortable, or awe-inspiring can reshape how people see the world, but they only help when leaders co-design them with their teams. Pushing people too far outside their comfort zone alienates them rather than growing them.

## Key Takeaways

- **Co-design team experiences before planning them.** Ask team members about their abilities, disabilities, and comfort levels first so the experience is safe and fits the group.
- **Stretch people only within their zone of proximity.** Choose challenges close enough to what people already know to grow them, not so far that they disengage.
- **Account for hierarchy and politics before asking people to struggle publicly.** Consider whether people feel safe being beginners in front of colleagues, especially across reporting lines.
- **Use learning a new skill to practice being a beginner.** Activities like learning a sport build comfort with continuous improvement and with being outside one's comfort zone.
- **Seek out experiences that offer new lenses, not just new tactics.** Pick events that expose teams to ideas they have never considered, even if the link to their work is not obvious at first.
