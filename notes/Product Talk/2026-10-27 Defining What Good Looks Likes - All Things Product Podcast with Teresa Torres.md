# Defining What Good Looks Likes - All Things Product Podcast with Teresa Torres & Petra Wille

**Source**: [Product Talk](https://www.producttalk.org/defining-what-good-looks-likes-all-things-product-podcast-with-teresa-torres-petra-wille/)
**Author**: Teresa Torres (featuring Petra Wille) | **Date**: Oct 27, 2026

---

## Summary

AI evals are not a new kind of problem; they are the same work product teams have always done in defining what "good" looks like and what outcomes they expect. Teams should measure customer and business value, not just technical performance, and define quality in their own context rather than outsourcing it to generic evals.

## Key Takeaways

- **Define "good" in your own context.** Write down what a successful customer outcome looks like for your product instead of relying on off-the-shelf eval suites, since the right answer depends on who the user is.
- **Build evals across three layers.** Measure technology performance, customer value, and business value together, so you don't stop at uptime, speed, or whether an answer was concise.
- **Treat "good" as the customer creating value.** Identify the specific action a customer takes that creates value (for example, sending a document in DocuSign) and measure whether users reach it, rather than tracking vanity metrics like MAU.
- **Let context change the definition.** Test whether a response fits the user's level, since a concise answer may be right for an expert and wrong for a beginner.
- **State your expectations before you test.** Write down what you expect to happen so you can recognize and learn from being wrong, and fix the underlying product or process before automating it with AI.
