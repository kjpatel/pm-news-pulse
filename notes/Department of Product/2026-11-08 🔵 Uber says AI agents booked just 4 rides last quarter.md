# 🔵 Uber says AI agents booked just 4 rides last quarter

**Source**: [Department of Product](https://departmentofproduct.substack.com/p/uber-says-ai-agents-booked-just-4)
**Author**: Rich Holmes | **Date**: Nov 08, 2026

## Summary

Uber reports that AI agents booked only 4 rides last quarter, a small result that raises the question of what good agentic UX requires. The piece pairs this with other AI platform news, including Siri opening to every app, Perplexity Team Mates, and voice in Muse.

## Key Takeaways

- **Measure agent adoption against real conversion, not hype.** Track how many transactions agents complete end to end, and compare that against the number of users who tried them, before investing further in an agent channel.
- **Diagnose where agent-assisted flows break down.** Look for steps where users drop off or switch back to the native app, since the low booking count suggests friction in the handoff, not just weak demand.
- **Design for agents as a user interface, not just an API.** Ask whether your product's key actions can be expressed clearly enough for an agent to complete them, and whether users can trust and verify what the agent did.
- **Watch platform-level distribution shifts.** Opening Siri to every app and launching new agent products change how users reach services, so plan for agents to become a discovery and access layer for your product.
