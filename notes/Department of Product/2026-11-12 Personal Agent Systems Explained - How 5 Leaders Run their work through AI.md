# Personal Agent Systems Explained - How 5 Leaders Run their work through AI Agents

**Source**: [Department of Product](https://departmentofproduct.substack.com/p/personal-agent-systems-explained)
**Author**: Rich Holmes | **Date**: Nov 12, 2026

---

## Summary

Leaders such as Coinbase's Brian Armstrong, Notion's COO, Linear's CEO, and Anthropic's Addy Osmani are organizing their work around personal agent systems, typically a lead agent per focus area overseen by a chief of staff agent. The article argues that agents as teammates are accelerating, so product teams should start experimenting with building their own versions.

## Key Takeaways

- **Organize agents by focus area:** Assign one always-running lead agent to each area of your product work, with its own sub-agents for tracing issues, testing, and chasing collaborators.
- **Add a chief of staff agent:** Create an oversight agent that tracks all lead agents and consolidates their findings so you can make decisions in one place instead of context switching.
- **Build a shared context vault:** Keep a central store of your context, such as notes, decisions, and priorities, that every agent you try can plug into.
- **Use adversarial agents for big decisions:** Set up an agent whose job is to challenge your reasoning before major product or business calls.
- **Start with one specific problem:** Pick a single recurring workflow, like handling bug complaints, and build a small personal agent system around it before expanding.
