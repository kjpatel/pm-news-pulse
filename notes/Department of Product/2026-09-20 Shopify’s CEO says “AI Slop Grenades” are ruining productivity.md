# Shopify’s CEO says “AI Slop Grenades” are ruining productivity

**Source**: [Department of Product](https://departmentofproduct.substack.com/p/shopifys-ceo-says-ai-slop-grenades)
**Author**: Rich Holmes | **Date**: Sep 20, 2026

---

## Summary

Shopify's CEO argues that low-quality, AI-generated output, which he calls "AI slop grenades," is hurting team productivity by pushing the work of checking and cleaning up that output onto colleagues. The piece also covers related product news, including ChatGPT Work for semantic data analysis, Claude Code Projects, and Snap's "anticipatory UX" bet.

## Key Takeaways

- **Review AI output before passing it to others.** Check drafts, analyses, and code for accuracy and completeness so colleagues don't absorb the cleanup cost.
- **Set a quality bar for AI-generated work.** Define what a finished, reviewable deliverable looks like on your team so volume doesn't crowd out substance.
- **Measure productivity by downstream impact, not output volume.** Track whether AI-assisted work reduces review cycles and rework rather than just counting documents or PRs produced.
- **Explore semantic data analysis tools for faster insight.** Test tools like ChatGPT Work on real datasets to see whether they shorten the path from question to validated answer.
- **Watch how product teams are designing for anticipation.** Follow experiments like Snap's "anticipatory UX" and consider where proactive features could reduce user effort in your own product.
