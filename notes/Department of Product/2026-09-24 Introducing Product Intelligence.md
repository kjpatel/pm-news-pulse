# Introducing Product Intelligence

**Source**: [Department of Product](https://departmentofproduct.substack.com/p/introducing-product-intelligence)
**Author**: Rich Holmes | **Date**: Sep 24, 2026

---

## Summary

Department of Product introduces a new approach that helps product teams turn product market intelligence into personalized product strategy. The article's teaser frames this as a shift from generic market research to strategy tailored to a specific team's context.

## Key Takeaways

- **Start with the market signals you already track.** Inventory the competitive, pricing, and customer-market data your team collects, and note which signals actually change product decisions.
- **Tailor intelligence to your own product context.** Filter market insights through your product's segment, stage, and goals before deciding what they mean for your roadmap.
- **Turn each insight into a specific strategic choice.** For every market trend you act on, write down the product bet it implies, the metric that would confirm or disprove it, and a review date.
- **Revisit the strategy on a regular cadence.** Schedule a recurring review of market intelligence against your strategy so the plan adjusts as the market moves rather than staying fixed after one planning cycle.
