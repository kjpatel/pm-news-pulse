# 🔵 Meta’s Muse comes to your keyring - but Amazon isn’t happy

**Source**: [Department of Product](https://departmentofproduct.substack.com/p/metas-muse-comes-to-your-keyring)
**Author**: Rich Holmes | **Date**: Sep 27, 2026

---

## Summary

Meta's Muse agent is expanding into credential management (the "keyring"), and Amazon is pushing back on it. The newsletter also covers Figma's CEO arguing that UIs won't disappear, YouTube and Spotify shipping algorithmic controls, and a new OpenAI mental health benchmark.

## Key Takeaways

- **Watch platform gatekeeping of AI agents:** Amazon's objection to Muse shows that platforms may restrict third-party agents that touch their ecosystems, so check whether your product's integrations depend on access a platform can revoke.
- **Design for both agent and human interfaces:** Figma's CEO argues UIs won't disappear, so build agent-accessible endpoints alongside the existing interface rather than assuming agents will replace the front end.
- **Give users algorithmic controls:** YouTube and Spotify shipping user-facing algorithm controls signals rising expectations, so consider adding visible ways for users to tune recommendations in your own product.
- **Benchmark wellbeing risks before shipping AI features:** OpenAI's new mental health benchmark suggests evaluating emotional and wellbeing failure modes as part of your launch criteria, not just accuracy or task completion.
