# Making Sense of the New Product Development Model

**Source**: [Department of Product](https://departmentofproduct.substack.com/p/making-sense-of-the-new-product-development)
**Author**: Rich Holmes | **Date**: Sep 22, 2026

---

## Summary

The article outlines five emerging themes in how product development is changing, illustrated with real-world examples and case studies from companies such as Spotify, Linear, Ramp, PostHog, and Square. The full argument isn't in the provided text, so this summary is based on the article's teaser.

## Key Takeaways

- **Study the concrete case studies rather than the abstract model.** Read the Spotify, Linear, Ramp, PostHog, and Square examples and note which specific practices your team could copy or adapt.
- **Map each of the five themes against your current process.** Identify where your team already follows each pattern and where it doesn't, then pick one gap to address this quarter.
- **Compare your development cadence with the examples.** Check how these companies structure iteration, release, and feedback loops, and benchmark your team's cycle time against them.
- **Pilot one new development practice before rolling it out widely.** Choose the theme with the clearest fit for your product, run it with one team for a few weeks, and measure the outcome before expanding it.
