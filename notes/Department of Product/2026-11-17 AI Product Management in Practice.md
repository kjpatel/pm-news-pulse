# AI Product Management in Practice

**Source**: [Department of Product](https://departmentofproduct.substack.com/p/ai-product-management-in-practice)
**Author**: Rich Holmes | **Date**: Nov 17, 2026

---

## Summary

The article examines how product management is changing with AI, drawing on 30+ real-world examples from companies like Spotify, Databricks, and Ramp, along with an analysis of 320 PM job ads. It frames the shift in PM work through what companies are actually hiring for and doing.

## Key Takeaways

- **Study the job market before retooling your skills:** Review current PM job postings, as the article's analysis of 320 ads shows which AI-related skills employers are asking for.
- **Benchmark your work against real company examples:** Compare how Spotify, Databricks, and Ramp are changing their product processes, and pick one practice to pilot on your own team.
- **Redefine your PM role around AI-era responsibilities:** Identify which tasks AI can now handle, and shift your time toward judgment, prioritization, and customer insight.
- **Build a personal portfolio of AI product work:** Ship at least one small AI-enabled feature or prototype and document the decisions you made, since hiring managers increasingly look for demonstrated practice.
