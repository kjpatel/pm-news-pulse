# Anthropic CEO warns about the dangers of a “fanatically devoted collective” of AI agents

**Source**: [Department of Product](https://departmentofproduct.substack.com/p/anthropic-ceo-warns-about-the-dangers)
**Author**: Rich Holmes | **Date**: Sep 13, 2026

---

## Summary

The article reports on an Anthropic CEO warning about the risks of a "fanatically devoted collective" of AI agents acting together. The issue also covers Meta's AI product progress and design considerations for the iPhone Duo.

## Key Takeaways

- **Govern agents as a group, not just one at a time.** Map which agents can coordinate, share context, or act on each other's outputs, and set limits on those interactions before scaling.
- **Define clear human oversight points.** Specify which agent actions require human approval, and set escalation paths for when agents disagree or act outside their intended scope.
- **Audit agent goals and incentives.** Check whether each agent's objective could push it toward unexpected or overly persistent behavior, and write explicit stop conditions.
- **Track Meta's AI product moves against your roadmap.** Compare its adoption and design choices with your own AI features to see where your product can differentiate.
- **Design for new device form factors early.** Start planning interaction patterns for the iPhone Duo now so your product is ready if the hardware gains traction.
