# The new UX of Mobile

**Source**: [Department of Product](https://departmentofproduct.substack.com/p/the-new-ux-of-mobile)
**Author**: Rich Holmes | **Date**: Nov 10, 2026

---

## Summary

Mobile app design is being reshaped by AI, foldable screens, and native rebuilds, which change what a phone app is expected to do. The article uses 35+ real-world examples from companies like Meta, Apple, Linear, Strava, and Superhuman to show these shifts.

## Key Takeaways

- **Audit your app's core jobs against AI capabilities.** Identify which multi-step tasks users currently do manually in your app and test whether an AI-assisted flow could complete them in fewer taps.
- **Design for foldables and variable screen sizes now.** Treat layout as responsive across fold states and tablet-like widths rather than as a fixed phone screen, so your app doesn't break as device form factors diversify.
- **Evaluate whether a native rebuild is worth the cost.** Compare the performance, gesture, and platform-integration gains of native code against the speed of shipping cross-platform, using competitors' examples as benchmarks.
- **Collect real-world UX examples before redesigning.** Build a swipe file of patterns from leading apps, organized by problem (navigation, onboarding, input, feedback), to ground design decisions in what users already recognize.
