# How to develop a Headless Product Strategy

**Source**: [Department of Product](https://departmentofproduct.substack.com/p/how-to-develop-a-headless-product)
**Author**: Rich Holmes | **Date**: Sep 15, 2026

---

## Summary

The article argues that product teams should not design only for human users, since AI agents are increasingly the ones interacting with software. It explores tactics and examples of headless product strategy, where capabilities are exposed for machines to use directly.

## Key Takeaways

- **Audit which product capabilities agents could reach:** List the core actions in your product and identify which ones an AI agent could perform through an API or tool interface without a human-facing screen.
- **Expose functionality through APIs and tools:** Make key workflows callable programmatically so agents and third-party systems can complete tasks on a user's behalf.
- **Design for machine users alongside human users:** Define how success, permissions, and error handling work when the actor is an agent rather than a person.
- **Treat the UI as one interface among several:** Evaluate whether your current UI roadmap is prioritizing screens over the underlying services that agents would need.
- **Study early headless examples before committing:** Review real examples of headless products to identify patterns you can test in a small pilot before a larger rebuild.
