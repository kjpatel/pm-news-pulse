# Live Voice Blueprints: 5 Features You Can Build with GPT-Live, Gemini Live and more

**Source**: [Department of Product](https://departmentofproduct.substack.com/p/live-voice-blueprints-5-features)
**Author**: Rich Holmes | **Date**: Oct 26, 2026

---

## Summary

The article introduces live voice and vision AI models and the product possibilities they unlock, drawing on demos from OpenAI, Google, Yelp, Twilio, HeyGen, and Block. It frames these models as a foundation for building new kinds of real-time, multimodal experiences.

## Key Takeaways

- **Prototype a real-time voice or vision flow:** Pick one customer task that currently requires typing or clicking and test a live voice or camera-based version with a small group of users.
- **Study the vendor demos before building:** Review the OpenAI, Google, Twilio, and HeyGen demos to see which interaction patterns (interruptions, turn-taking, screen sharing) are already proven.
- **Define latency and interruption requirements early:** Live models change expectations for response speed and barge-in behavior, so set these thresholds before committing to an architecture.
- **Plan for consent and privacy from day one:** Continuous audio or video capture raises trust issues, so decide what is recorded, retained, and shown to users before launch.
- **Measure task completion, not conversation length:** Track whether the voice or vision feature actually resolves the user's goal faster than the existing flow.
