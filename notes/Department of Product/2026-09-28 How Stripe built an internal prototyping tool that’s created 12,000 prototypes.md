# How Stripe built an internal prototyping tool that’s created 12,000 prototypes so far

**Source**: [Department of Product](https://departmentofproduct.substack.com/p/how-stripe-built-an-internal-prototyping)
**Author**: Rich Holmes | **Date**: Sep 28, 2026

---

## Summary

Stripe's internal prototyping tool has produced 12,000 prototypes, and the article explains how product teams can build a similar tool of their own. It also surveys other new design tools and technologies that are changing how product work gets done.

## Key Takeaways

- **Identify the prototyping bottleneck first.** Map where ideas stall between concept and testable prototype on your team, then build internal tooling around that specific gap rather than adopting tools generically.
- **Build a lightweight internal prototyping tool.** Start with a narrow scope, such as generating prototypes from your own design system and components, so anyone on the team can produce a clickable prototype quickly.
- **Treat prototype volume as a learning metric.** Track how many prototypes get created and how many inform decisions, since a high count signals that teams are experimenting cheaply and testing more ideas.
- **Evaluate new design tools against your existing workflow.** Before adopting an AI design tool, test it on a real project and compare its output quality and handoff effort with your current process.
