# How Uber transformed its engineering process to reduce token spend by 52%

**Source**: [Department of Product](https://departmentofproduct.substack.com/p/how-uber-transformed-its-engineering)
**Author**: Rich Holmes | **Date**: Sep 08, 2026

---

## Summary

The article argues that the era of 'tokenmaxxing' (spending freely on AI tokens) is over and that teams need to cut token spend deliberately. It cites real-world reduction examples from Spotify, Zapier, Stripe, and others, including Uber's 52% reduction through changes to its engineering process.

## Key Takeaways

- **Treat token spend as an engineering metric.** Track token consumption per workflow or feature so you can see where cost is concentrated before trying to reduce it.
- **Restructure the engineering process, not just the prompts.** Uber's 52% reduction came from changing how engineering work flows, so look for process-level changes (scoping, task routing, review steps) before tuning individual prompts.
- **Benchmark against peers' reduction playbooks.** Review the Spotify, Zapier, and Stripe examples to identify patterns you can adapt, such as which tasks to route to cheaper models or where to cut redundant context.
- **Set explicit token budgets per team or project.** Ending tokenmaxxing means replacing unlimited usage with budgets that force trade-offs between cost and output quality.
