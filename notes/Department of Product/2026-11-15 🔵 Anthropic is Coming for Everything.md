## Summary

This edition of the Department of Product newsletter teases a fast-moving AI landscape: Anthropic expanding into more markets, Gemini 4 Hydrogen approaching top-model quality at a fraction of the cost, HubSpot's founders building "Headless HubSpot," and ChatGPT adding a payments wallet for agents. ## Key Takeaways

- **Re-benchmark your model choices against cost:** When a near-frontier model like Gemini 4 Hydrogen reaches comparable quality at about a fifth of the price, run your key evals on it and compare cost per successful task before locking in a vendor.
- **Plan for agents as payers and buyers:** With ChatGPT adding a wallet for agents, map which product flows an agent could complete on a user's behalf, and define spending limits, approvals, and audit trails now.
- **Evaluate headless and API-first strategy:** HubSpot's founders returning to build "Headless HubSpot" signals that CRM value may shift toward data and APIs that agents consume. Review whether your product's core value is accessible through clean, well-documented interfaces.
