## Summary

Claude Opus 5 is technically superior but requires strategic system prompts to work effectively; the model was trained for autonomous long-horizon agents at the expense of collaborative interaction, but can be 'healed' with specific instruction blocks that restore its utility.

## Key Takeaways

- **Add explicit instruction blocks** to your CLAUDE.md file that clarify when Opus 5 should act autonomously versus ask for permission, preventing the model from becoming overly cautious or seeking unnecessary approval.
- **Delete your old system prompts selectively** rather than completely starting fresh—keep strategic context, preferences, and decisions you've already made while removing outdated instructions that conflict with Opus 5's new training.
- **Define task completion clearly** by specifying that 'done' means all requested items are delivered, not partial completion or reports about future work, forcing the model to finish what it started.
