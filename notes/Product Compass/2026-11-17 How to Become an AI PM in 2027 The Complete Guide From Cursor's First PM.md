# How to Become an AI PM in 2027: The Complete Guide From Cursor's First PM

**Source**: [Product Compass](https://www.productcompass.pm/p/how-to-become-an-ai-product-manager)
**Author**: Pawel Huryn (featuring Rohan Varma) | **Date**: Nov 17, 2026

---

## Summary

Rohan Varma, Cursor's first PM and former Codex product lead at OpenAI, argues that landing an AI PM role in 2027 depends on mastering eight practical skills rather than copying the career paths of frontier-lab PMs. The core idea is that AI PMs are hired to solve real problems with AI where it creates strategic advantage, not to use AI for its own sake.

## Key Takeaways

- **Start with the customer problem, not the model**: Talk to the people doing the work, find the real bottleneck (for example, finding policies rather than writing replies), and only then choose an AI approach.
- **Define measurable success before building**: Set concrete targets such as cutting time on policy questions by 50% while keeping error rates below 1%, and validate baselines with the team.
- **Test model behavior on five dimensions**: Evaluate tokens, context window, tool calling, latency, and inference cost across candidate models using a realistic test set, such as 50 real customer questions.
- **Design for verification and control**: Build AI features like a support copilot that drafts answers with citations and lets humans verify, edit, and approve before anything is sent.
- **Build portfolio projects that prove you can ship**: Show hands-on AI product work, since few senior PMs have actually shipped a working agent or model-based product.
