## Summary

Agentic coding is now largely solved, which has left engineers unsure what their skills are worth and pushed product people to become more technical so they can use agents effectively. The article (only its introduction was provided) introduces a set of 43 free skills meant to take a product from first idea to paying users.

## Key Takeaways

- **Build with agents directly:** Start prototyping product ideas yourself with coding agents instead of only writing specs for engineers, since agentic coding now handles much of the implementation work.
- **Use the skills as a playbook:** Work through the free skills in sequence, from idea validation to shipping, so you cover the full path from first idea to paying users rather than picking tools ad hoc.
- **Close the technical gap deliberately:** Identify which technical concepts limit your ability to direct agents, and learn enough to review and steer what they produce.
- **Watch where engineering skills still matter:** Treat engineers' concerns as a signal about which judgment and craft agents don't replace, and keep those skills in your own toolkit.
