# Building a Better SuperMe, and Raising a $20 Million Series A

**Source**: [Casey Accidental](https://www.caseyaccidental.com/p/building-a-better-superme)
**Author**: Casey Winters | **Date**: Oct 29, 2026

---

## Summary

SuperMe, a network that turns professionals' shared work into conversational profiles, announces a $20M Series A and new features: multi-profile group chats and access from ChatGPT and Claude. The article argues that experienced operators should be recognized for their knowledge without needing to become full-time creators.

## Key Takeaways

- **Bring multiple perspectives into one challenge.** When facing a hard decision, start a group chat with several relevant profiles and compare where they agree and disagree, rather than relying on a single advisor.
- **Use expert input where you already work.** Query relevant profiles from inside your ChatGPT or Claude conversation, so feedback sits alongside the context you've already built, such as a product strategy doc.
- **Pressure-test experiments with peer operators.** Ask the group how to interpret mixed metric results and whether to roll out a change, including the tradeoffs you may be overlooking.
- **Get feedback on your pitch before fundraising.** Ask profiles to identify the weakest parts of your deck and the questions you should be ready to answer.
- **Turn your work into a discoverable knowledge asset.** Build a profile from the work you choose to share (notes, docs, published posts) so your experience can answer questions and attract relevant conversations without live calls or content creation.
