# How to steal your way to a hit product, with Mark Pincus (founder, Zynga)

**Source**: [Product Growth](https://www.news.aakashg.com/p/how-to-steal-your-way-to-a-hit-product)
**Author**: Aakash Gupta (featuring Mark Pincus) | **Date**: Oct 26, 2026

---

## Summary

Mark Pincus argues that every great product starts as a copy, and that the skill lies in separating proven elements worth copying exactly from the single new idea a launch should test. He adds that early in a platform shift, teams should ship less new and validate demand before building the full product.

## Key Takeaways

- **Copy what's proven click by click:** Map the closest competitor's experience at a granular level and replicate every element that already works for your audience on your platform, so your launch only measures your one novel idea.
- **Apply the 10-out-of-10 test before calling anything 'better':** Only label an idea better if 10 of 10 users of the existing product would agree it is better; otherwise classify it as new and treat it as an experiment.
- **Limit new features early in a platform shift:** When the market is still forming, prioritize improving proven patterns with AI rather than inventing new features, and write down which part of your AI product is truly new so you aren't running multiple experiments in one launch.
- **Test demand before building the product:** Use a teardown, concept batch, and a one-page landing site with paid ads to measure click-through rate against a bar you set in advance, before committing to a full MVP.
- **Plan for most new ideas to fail:** Assume your own new idea is probably wrong, avoid letting failing ideas linger, and give real reverence to new features that prove themselves in the market, since they then become proven.
