# How to Ace PM Interview Presentations and Homework in the AI Era

**Source**: [Product Growth](https://www.news.aakashg.com/p/pm-take-home-assignment-playbook)
**Author**: Aakash Gupta (featuring Sumant Malhotra) | **Date**: Oct 30, 2026

---

## Summary

PM take-home assignments and presentations are common across AI-native companies, scale-ups, and large tech firms, because they work as a filter and a strong predictor of job performance. Candidates succeed by producing judgment and insight that AI cannot generate for anyone else given the same prompt.

## Key Takeaways

- **Tailor each homework response to the company's specific format.** PRDs, strategy docs, case studies, past-project walkthroughs, and AI prototypes each call for different approaches, so don't reuse one template across companies.
- **Study the company's existing internal docs and aim to beat them.** Hiring panels compare your document against what already circulates internally, so match their standards and then raise the bar.
- **Find an insight that only you could produce.** Pasting the prompt into ChatGPT or Claude yields the same answer for everyone, so build your response around original judgment and specific experience.
- **Put visible effort into research, writing, and editing.** Reviewers compare thoroughness, organization, and proofreading across candidates and may run submissions through AI-detection tools, so the work should read as clearly hand-written.
- **Ask the recruiter what the homework is for before you start.** Knowing whether it tests a PRD, strategy, or prototyping ability tells you where to spend your time and which skills to demonstrate.
