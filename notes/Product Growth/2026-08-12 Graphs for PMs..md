# Graphs for PMs.

**Source**: [Product Growth](https://www.news.aakashg.com/p/graphs)
**Author**: Aakash Gupta | **Date**: Aug 12, 2026

---

## Summary

Product managers should learn graph databases and structures, as they represent the next evolution beyond loops for handling complex data relationships in AI-native product work.

## Key Takeaways

- **Master graph fundamentals** - Graphs are the natural next step after loops for PMs and are surprisingly easy to learn and prompt effectively.
- **Understand relationship mapping** - Graphs excel at representing interconnected data that loops cannot efficiently handle, making them essential for modern product architecture.
- **Adopt graphs into your PM toolkit** - As Peter Steinberger suggests, staying current with graph technology is now a practical requirement for competitive product management.
