# I'm adding Airtable to my Bundle

**Source**: [Product Growth](https://www.news.aakashg.com/p/airtable-bundle)
**Author**: Aakash Gupta | **Date**: Jul 24, 2026

---

## Summary

Aakash is adding Airtable to his Bundle subscription, offering $27,778 in AI tools and software for $150/year, and provides a comprehensive guide to using Airtable as a centralized platform for product management work.

## Key Takeaways

- **Use Airtable as your product OS** - Implement it as a centralized system combining roadmapping, task management, automations, and feedback tracking rather than maintaining scattered spreadsheets and documents across tools.
- **Start with AI-generated templates** - Leverage Airtable's AI agent to build a basic product roadmapping system by uploading your current roadmap export, then layer on automations and forms for stakeholder intake.
- **Connect your entire product stack** - Link Airtable with Claude MCP, automations (email triggers, webhooks), Kanban boards, and forms to create a self-updating system that requires less manual maintenance as your company scales.
- **Evaluate when you need it** - Only transition to Airtable from sheets when you reach scale and need standardization; smaller scrappy teams may not require the maintenance overhead of a structured platform.
