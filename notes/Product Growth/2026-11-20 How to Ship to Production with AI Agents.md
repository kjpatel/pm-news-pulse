# How to Ship to Production with AI Agents

**Source**: [Product Growth](https://www.news.aakashg.com/p/ai-agents-ship-to-production)
**Author**: Aakash Gupta (featuring Shuhao Zhang) | **Date**: Nov 20, 2026

---

## Summary

Most AI PMs still aren't shipping AI features to production, and this episode, featuring TinyFish co-founder and CPO Shuhao Zhang, argues that PMs can close that gap by adopting Meta-quality engineering systems and workflows. The companion newsletter packages a copyable scaffolding setup for shipping to production.

## Key Takeaways

- **Share the episode with your engineers** as a starting point for proposing a production-ready way of building AI features, rather than prototyping indefinitely.
- **Copy the scaffolding from the newsletter** and run it on a real bug from your own product to test whether a PM-driven shipping workflow holds up before rolling it out to your team.
- **Take on the new PM role of owning the shipping pipeline** by defining the standards, checks, and regression safeguards that AI-generated changes must pass before release.
- **Benchmark your team against the 50/50 split** by asking whether your AI features are actually reaching production users, and identify the specific blockers if they aren't.
