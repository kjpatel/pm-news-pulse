# How to Survive AI as a SaaS Company

**Source**: [Product Growth](https://www.news.aakashg.com/p/can-saas-survive-the-ai-era)
**Author**: Aakash Gupta (featuring Linda Tong) | **Date**: Nov 10, 2026

---

## Summary

AI gives customers a free first version of most software, so SaaS companies survive by building the work that comes after publish, serving a narrow buyer AI can't serve, and turning AI platforms into a distribution channel. The article argues that companies also need a product team structured for speed, with every PM acting as an AI PM.

## Key Takeaways

- **Push up your growth rate to push up your multiple.** Public SaaS companies with multiples above 15x grew a median 34%, versus 11% for those under 5x, so growth is the lever to track and report on.
- **Build the work AI leaves behind.** Identify the post-first-draft tasks (editing, governance, collaboration, publishing, ongoing iteration) that AI tools handle poorly, and make those your core product.
- **Pick one buyer and go deep.** Pull usage data by job-to-be-done, map job titles to each job, and choose the job many titles share as your ICP before expanding to adjacent users.
- **Treat ChatGPT and Claude as your top of funnel.** Make it easy for users to start in an AI site builder or agent and bring the work into your product, then measure success by publish rate and time to first publish.
- **Restructure the team around prototypes and taste.** Have PMs build first prototypes in code, staff small pods of two or three engineers, a designer, and a PM, and measure outcomes over output.
