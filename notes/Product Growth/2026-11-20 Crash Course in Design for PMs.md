# Crash Course in Design for PMs

**Source**: [Product Growth](https://www.news.aakashg.com/p/design-sense)
**Author**: Aakash Gupta | **Date**: Nov 20, 2026

---

## Summary

PMs increasingly design through AI prototypes, yet AI tools still produce poor design, so PMs need real design sense to judge and fix what AI generates. The article offers a crash course covering how designers learn, core design concepts, training drills, and how to direct AI tools and work with designers.

## Key Takeaways

- **Critique layout before aesthetics:** Check whether the most important output sits above the fold, whether the primary input is given enough space, and whether rankings visibly match their underlying numbers.
- **Build a design vocabulary through a fixed sequence:** Learn to see first, then train your eye with drills, then apply it to AI tools, and finally work with designers, following the order designers themselves use.
- **Don't trust AI-generated design by default:** Test AI output against design principles before shipping, since single-prompt builds from tools like Claude Code can look functional while failing basic hierarchy and clarity.
- **Use the PRD-beside-input layout pattern:** When building AI prototypes, place outputs next to their inputs, show the scoring logic, and collapse raw inputs into compact summaries so users can follow the logic.
- **Set aside 3-5 hours for the full crash course:** Work through the 12 core design concepts and drills in one sitting, then practice on a real prototype to build durable design judgment.
