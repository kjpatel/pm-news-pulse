# I spent months building you an automated job search agent

**Source**: [Product Growth](https://www.news.aakashg.com/p/job-search-agent)
**Author**: Aakash Gupta | **Date**: Nov 13, 2026

---

## Summary

The author introduces a paid, AI-powered Job Search OS that uses browser and computer-use agents to find roles, tailor resumes, draft connection notes, and prep interview materials, while keeping the human in charge of final edits and sends. He argues this cuts a roughly 3-hour job search workflow to under 30 minutes and frames the product as a $49 tool worth far more than a typical job-switch raise.

## Key Takeaways

- **Write your own connection notes.** The agent drafts them, but you should personalize each one and send it yourself to keep outreach authentic and avoid spam-like behavior.
- **Humanize the resume before sending.** The system can submit tailored resumes as-is, but rewriting them in your own words first is recommended for stronger applications.
- **Apply through company sites, not job boards.** Targeting postings at specific employers, rather than mass-applying, is presented as the way to get responses and avoid LinkedIn bans.
- **Filter postings for visa sponsorship before applying.** Check each role's sponsorship language and verify which employers actually filed H-1B petitions last year to avoid wasted applications.
- **Move up company tiers deliberately.** Use target-company intelligence to aim for higher-paying employers rather than applying broadly, since a job switch often brings a 10-15% raise.
