# Partnering with Preview: Lights, Inference, Action

**Source**: [Sequoia Capital](https://sequoiacap.com/article/partnering-with-preview-lights-inference-action/)
**Author**: Sequoia Capital (featuring Sonya Huang and James Flynn) | **Date**: Aug 12, 2026

---

## Summary

Preview is an AI-native video creation platform that brings the seamless, AI-integrated workflow of modern coding tools to professional video production, enabling teams to generate, track, and collaborate on video assets at scale.

## Key Takeaways

- **Adopt AI-native tooling** - Just as Cursor transformed coding workflows, Preview represents the necessary paradigm shift for video production; legacy editing pipelines that bolt together separate generation, review, and tracking tools are becoming obsolete.
- **Build complete audit trails** - The platform's ability to track every character, location, and prop with full provenance records addresses critical legal and production needs that fragmented workflows cannot solve.
- **Focus on inference economics** - With video models now expressive enough to replace traditional cameras, the bottleneck has shifted from technical capability to managing token inference costs and creative ideation at scale.
