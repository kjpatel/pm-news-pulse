# Partnering with Preview: Lights, Inference, Action

**Source**: [Sequoia Capital](https://www.sequoiacap.com/article/partnering-with-preview-lights-inference-action)
**Author**: Sequoia Capital (featuring Sonya Huang and James Flynn) | **Date**: Aug 12, 2026

---

## Summary

Preview is building an AI-native video creation platform that serves as the missing 'Cursor for video,' enabling professional creators to seamlessly integrate generative video models into a unified workspace where generation, review, and production tracking happen in one collaborative environment.

## Key Takeaways

- **Adopt AI-native tools designed for your workflow.** Video creators should transition from legacy editing pipelines to integrated AI-native platforms like Preview that combine timeline editing, ideation, and production tracking in one space.
- **Establish complete asset provenance records.** Track every character, location, and prop with full records of how assets were created to ensure legal clearance and maintain quality control across AI-generated content.
- **Leverage multiplayer collaboration frameworks.** Use collaborative workspaces that enable both human creatives and AI models to work together seamlessly, rather than bolting model outputs onto disconnected applications.
- **Position yourself at the inference bottleneck.** Build platforms where creative decisions and records are centralized—this is where value accrues as the 'single source of truth' from which all downstream inference flows.
