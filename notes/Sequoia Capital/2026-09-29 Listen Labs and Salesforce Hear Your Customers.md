# Listen Labs and Salesforce: Hear Your Customers

**Source**: [Sequoia Capital](https://www.sequoiacap.com/article/listen-labs-and-salesforce-hear-your-customers)
**Author**: Sequoia Capital | **Date**: Sep 29, 2026

---

## Summary

Listen Labs built an LLM-powered market research and customer experience platform that replaces slow, annual surveys with continuous, real-time customer interviews and synthesis. Its acquisition by Salesforce extends that capability across Salesforce's Marketing and Service Clouds, letting companies simulate and act on customer behavior.

## Key Takeaways

- **Replace periodic research with continuous listening:** Run short customer interviews monthly or weekly instead of waiting a year for a formal study, so product decisions reflect current customer needs.
- **Use language-based AI for research work:** Market research is built on asking questions and listening to answers, so LLM-led interviews and synthesis are a strong fit for gathering and analyzing customer feedback.
- **Build a product that users evangelize:** Listen Labs spread through Sequoia organically because the product was loved, so prioritize craft and quality, which drives word-of-mouth adoption more than sales effort.
- **Turn customer insight into simulation and action:** Use customer data to model what customers will do and why, then feed those insights directly into marketing, service, and product workflows.
