# Partnering with Catalyst: Turning Ideas into Trades

**Source**: [Sequoia Capital](https://www.sequoiacap.com/article/partnering-with-catalyst-turning-ideas-into-trades)
**Author**: Sequoia Capital | **Date**: Oct 08, 2026

---

## Summary

Catalyst, a startup backed by Sequoia's seed round, uses AI agents so users can describe an investment thesis in plain language and have the agents analyze market signals, choose a path across platforms, and backtest and deploy the strategy within set safety limits. The article argues that finance should start from user intent rather than market plumbing, and that the hard part is bringing users along with clear understanding.

## Key Takeaways

- **Describe the thesis, not the trade:** Frame an investment idea as a plain-language thesis with a specific trigger (e.g., a crop estimate or rainfall threshold) and let the tooling handle execution details.
- **Define limits before deploying:** Set explicit risk limits and safety controls, and backtest a strategy before letting any agent act on it with real capital.
- **Treat comprehension as a product requirement:** Build in questions, feedback, and explanations of options and implications so users understand what they are approving, not just what the agent did.
- **Use agents to close the expertise gap:** Individuals without institutional trading skills can use agents to access strategies that were previously available only to experts or through paid managers.
- **Watch for the orchestration layer:** Evaluate platforms that coordinate signals, venues, and execution across markets, since that is where the article sees the biggest opportunity in agent-driven finance.
