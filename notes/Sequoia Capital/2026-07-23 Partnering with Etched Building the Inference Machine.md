# Partnering with Etched: Building the Inference Machine

**Source**: [Sequoia Capital](https://www.sequoiacap.com/article/partnering-with-etched-building-the-inference-machine)
**Author**: Sequoia Capital | **Date**: Jul 23, 2026

---

## Summary

Etched has developed the first post-ChatGPT-era custom silicon for inference, building cluster-scale systems optimized for throughput and latency across diverse AI models. Sequoia is leading their $300M Series C at a $10B valuation, betting that Etched has created not just a great inference machine, but the capability to continuously build better ones.

## Key Takeaways

- **Design for clusters, not chips**: Etched's breakthrough was recognizing that the unit of compute has evolved from individual chips to racks to clusters, and architecting inference systems at this scale with innovations like low-voltage inference and cluster-scale memory.
- **Production is the product**: Separate yourself from competitors through relentless execution—Etched opened a San Jose lab, established a Taiwan office colocated with suppliers, and shipped production-ready silicon in 2026, proving their competitive advantage through tangible results.
- **Build the machine that builds machines**: Focus on creating scalable processes and architectural principles that allow you to iterate faster than model development cycles, enabling you to ship successive generations of improvements rather than being locked into a single design.
- **Question every assumption in hardware design**: Etched's low-voltage inference breakthrough came from challenging what was considered possible, suggesting hardware startups should systematically interrogate conventional wisdom about physics constraints and operating parameters.
- **Hire outliers with sustained execution ability**: Recruit founders and teams "visionary enough to create the right designs, young enough to move fast, proven enough to have chips in production, and built to do this for decades," not just for a single product cycle.
