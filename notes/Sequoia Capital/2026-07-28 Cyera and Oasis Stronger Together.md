# Cyera and Oasis: Stronger Together

**Source**: [Sequoia Capital](https://www.sequoiacap.com/article/cyera-and-oasis-stronger-together)
**Author**: Sequoia Capital | **Date**: Jul 28, 2026

---

## Summary

Cyera and Oasis Security are merging to create a comprehensive AI security platform that combines data security with agentic identity and access management, leveraging complementary technologies and Cyera's enterprise GTM capabilities to become the default security layer for AI-powered enterprises.

## Key Takeaways

- **Identify market gaps early**: Oasis spotted the shift from human to non-human identities (API keys, service accounts, agent credentials) three years before it became a board-level CISO priority, allowing first-mover advantage in agentic access management.
- **Combine complementary solutions**: Merge products that cover different parts of the same problem—Cyera handles data discovery and risk assessment while Oasis secures the identities accessing that data, creating an unbeatable end-to-end offering.
- **Leverage partner GTM engines**: Use an acquiring company's mature enterprise sales infrastructure (Cyera's decade-scale GTM team) to accelerate market penetration for a complementary product that would otherwise take years to build equivalent sales capacity.
- **Build for the next paradigm**: Design security solutions around emerging AI agent architectures and non-human identity patterns rather than retrofitting legacy human-identity tools to new use cases.
- **Spot founder talent from proven talent pools**: Israeli technical leadership programs like Talpiot and Unit 8200 have consistently produced disproportionate cybersecurity founders—use institutional knowledge of talent sources to identify high-potential teams.
