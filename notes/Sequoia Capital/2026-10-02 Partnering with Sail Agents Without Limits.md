# Partnering with Sail: Agents Without Limits

**Source**: [Sequoia Capital](https://www.sequoiacap.com/article/partnering-with-sail-agents-without-limits)
**Author**: Sequoia Capital | **Date**: Oct 02, 2026

---

## Summary

Sail is building an inference stack and runtime designed for AI agents that run for hours, days, or weeks, arguing that agents need cheap, flexible, and persistent compute to reach their potential. The article presents Sail's work as the token factory of the future, aimed at making intelligence abundant.

## Key Takeaways

- **Rethink inference for long-running agents:** Design infrastructure for agents that think for hours or days and consume billions of tokens per task, optimizing for cost per task rather than just latency per request.
- **Squeeze more output from every chip:** Rebuild the inference stack to extract maximum throughput from each accelerator, and adopt new hardware early instead of waiting for it to mature.
- **Route work across providers and idle capacity:** Distribute workloads intelligently across providers so nothing stalls, and tap underused or older data-center clusters that others overlook to cut costs.
- **Pay only for active compute:** Use persistent sandboxes that scale CPU and memory on demand so agents waiting on events or tool calls cost almost nothing while idle.
- **Partner early with infrastructure teams:** Look for teams with deep performance engineering backgrounds when building agent infrastructure, since the bottleneck is increasingly compute efficiency.
