# Partnering with Mecka: Bringing AI to the Physical World

**Source**: [Sequoia Capital](https://www.sequoiacap.com/article/partnering-with-mecka)
**Author**: Sequoia Capital | **Date**: Oct 07, 2026

---

## Summary

Sequoia argues that bringing AI into the physical world will be as significant as digital AI, but it requires harder infrastructure: physical training data must be captured before it can be learned from, and robots must operate in messy real-world environments. They back Mecka because it has built an integrated data, hardware, operations, software, and deployment platform that can scale regardless of how the data or robot form factors change.

## Key Takeaways

- **Treat data capture as a prerequisite, not an afterthought.** Robotics has no internet-scale corpus of movements and sensory data, so teams should plan to collect multi-camera video, hand and body tracking, and sensor streams before attempting to train on them.
- **Build all the required capabilities at once, not one at a time.** Training data must be high volume, high diversity, high quality, fast, and affordable together, so evaluate whether your operation can meet all of these constraints at scale rather than excelling at only one.
- **Watch for the next wave before it arrives.** Mecka's research muscle identifies what robotics will require ahead of time, so founders should invest in forecasting the data and hardware needs of the next phase of their market.
- **Design the system to scale across changing inputs.** Because the form factor of data and robots may shift, prioritize platforms whose outputs stay consistent for partners even as the underlying hardware or data type changes.
- **Pursue high-barrier physical markets for durable moats.** Where scaling is hard, a team that solves the operational complexity can build a large, defensible business, so look for markets where difficulty to execute is itself the source of advantage.
