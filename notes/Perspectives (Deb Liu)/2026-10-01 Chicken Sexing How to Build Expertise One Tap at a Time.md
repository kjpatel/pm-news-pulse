## Summary

The article argues that intuition and expertise matter more than ever in an AI-driven world, using the chicken-sexing analogy to show how deep expertise is built through many small, repeated judgment calls rather than explicit rules. The provided text is only the title and one line, so this summary is inferred from that limited content.

## Key Takeaways

- **Practice judgment through many small decisions:** Make repeated, quick calls in your domain and check the outcome each time, since expertise accumulates one tap at a time.
- **Log your reasoning and outcomes:** Record why you made each call and what happened, so you can refine your intuition instead of relying on memory.
- **Seek rapid, high-quality feedback loops:** Shorten the gap between a decision and its result so your pattern recognition improves faster.
- **Don't outsource judgment to AI entirely:** Use AI output as a second opinion, and form your own assessment first to keep your expertise sharp.
- **Treat intuition as a trainable skill:** Study examples from experts who perform well, then deliberately practice spotting the same patterns.
