# Job Search: Be the Signal in the Noise

**Source**: [Perspectives (Deb Liu)](https://debliu.substack.com/p/job-search-be-the-signal-in-the-noise)
**Author**: Deb Liu | **Date**: Sep 23, 2026

---

## Summary

The article argues that job seekers can stand out in a crowded market by making small, deliberate changes to how they approach their search, rather than by sending more applications. The article body was not included, so this summary is based only on the title and subtitle.

## Key Takeaways

- **Audit your search approach before adding volume.** Review where your applications, outreach, and networking efforts currently go, and cut or refine the channels that produce little response.
- **Tailor one signal per application.** Pick one concrete accomplishment or insight that matches each role's specific problem and lead your resume and cover note with it.
- **Make your work visible to people who can refer you.** Share short, specific examples of your thinking, such as a post, a project write-up, or a useful note, so hiring managers recognize you before you apply.
- **Follow up with a short, specific message.** Reference a detail from the role or your earlier conversation and ask a focused question, which is more likely to get a reply than a generic check-in.
- **Track results weekly and adjust.** Log applications, replies, and interviews, then change one variable at a time to see which small adjustment actually improves your response rate.
