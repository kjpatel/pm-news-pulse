## Summary

Based only on the title and subtitle provided, the article argues that relying on AI to do your thinking carries hidden costs, such as losing ownership of your ideas and judgment. The full article body was not included, so this summary is inferred from the headline.

## Key Takeaways

- **Draft your own first version.** Write the core argument or outline before opening an AI tool so your reasoning stays yours and AI supports rather than replaces it.
- **Check every AI-generated claim before you publish it.** Verify facts, sources, and conclusions so the final work reflects your judgment, not the model's guesses.
- **Rewrite AI output in your own voice.** Edit drafts until every sentence reflects what you actually believe, which keeps the work authentically yours.
- **Schedule regular no-AI thinking time.** Set aside blocks for working through problems unaided to keep your reasoning skills sharp.
- **Keep a record of your decisions and their reasoning.** Note why you chose a direction so you can explain and defend the work, not just the output.
