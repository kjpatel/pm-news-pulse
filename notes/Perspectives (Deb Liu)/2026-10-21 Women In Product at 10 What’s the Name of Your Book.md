# Women In Product at 10: What’s the Name of Your Book?

**Source**: [Perspectives (Deb Liu)](https://debliu.substack.com/p/women-in-product-at-10-whats-the)
**Author**: Deb Liu | **Date**: Oct 21, 2026

---

## Summary

The article reflects on Women in Product's 10th anniversary conference, where a single prompt, "What's the name of your book?", prompted attendees to look at their own professional stories and identities. The piece suggests that reflective, personal framing can make a community event feel like a mirror rather than a showcase.

## Key Takeaways

- **Frame reflection around a concrete prompt:** Asking people to name their own "book" gives them a specific, low-pressure way to examine their career story, so consider using similar prompts in team retros or mentorship sessions.
- **Name the story you want to be known for:** Articulating a single, memorable title for your professional chapter helps you communicate your value and direction to peers and leaders.
- **Use community events for self-examination, not only networking:** Design gatherings that invite attendees to reflect on their own experiences, which builds deeper connections than standard panel formats.
- **Share your own answer first:** Going first with a personal, vulnerable reflection lowers the barrier for others to open up, whether you're leading a session or a one-on-one conversation.
