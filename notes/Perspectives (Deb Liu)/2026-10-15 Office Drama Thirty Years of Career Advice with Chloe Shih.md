## Key Takeaways

- **Treat office conflict as a leadership skill to learn deliberately.** Name the friction you see in a team, then decide who needs to act on it and by when instead of letting it linger.
- **Use decades of pattern-level experience to shape your advice.** Before coaching someone, identify which workplace situations recur across teams and companies, and tailor guidance to those patterns.
- **Look for lessons from people who have led under pressure.** Ask senior leaders how they handled a hard moment, and record the specific decisions they made rather than only their general principles.
- **Apply leadership lessons to your own team this month.** Pick one lesson from the conversation and test it in a single concrete situation, such as a difficult one-on-one or a stalled project.
