# The Doors You Can’t See Yet

**Source**: [Perspectives (Deb Liu)](https://debliu.substack.com/p/the-doors-you-cant-see-yet)
**Author**: Deb Liu | **Date**: Nov 19, 2026

---

## Summary

The article argues for deliberately keeping your options open, treating unexpected paths and future possibilities as valuable rather than as distractions from a fixed plan. The full text was not included, so this summary is based only on the title and subtitle.

## Key Takeaways

- **Schedule time to explore without a fixed goal.** Set aside a recurring block each month to learn about adjacent roles, industries, or skills, so you spot opportunities before you need them.
- **Avoid committing too early to a single narrow path.** Before accepting a role or project, ask what doors it closes and what it makes easier, and prefer options that preserve future flexibility.
- **Keep a running list of people and ideas that could open doors.** Note contacts, projects, and curiosities that don't fit your current plan, and revisit the list quarterly to see which ones have become relevant.
- **Build transferable skills that work across several fields.** Prioritize capabilities like writing, analysis, and communication that remain useful if your direction changes.
