## Summary

This personal essay recounts a colonoscopy, a subsequent surgery, and an unexpected complication, and appears to reflect on what being sick reveals about being well. The excerpt provided is very short, so this summary is based mainly on the title and opening line.

## Key Takeaways

- **Keep preventive screenings on schedule.** Book routine checkups and recommended screenings like colonoscopies before symptoms appear, since early detection gives you more options.
- **Ask your care team what complications to watch for.** Before a procedure, request a specific list of warning signs and who to call if they appear, so an unexpected issue gets caught early.
- **Notice what illness changes in your priorities.** After a health scare, list the work commitments and habits that matter least and deliberately drop or delegate at least one of them.
- **Build a recovery buffer into your calendar.** Schedule reduced workloads for weeks after a procedure rather than assuming you'll return to full speed immediately.
- **Tell someone you trust what you're going through.** Share your medical timeline with a friend, family member, or colleague who can check in, help with logistics, and notice when something seems wrong.
