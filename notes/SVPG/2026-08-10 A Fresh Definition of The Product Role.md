# A Fresh Definition of The Product Role

**Source**: [SVPG](https://www.svpg.com/a-fresh-definition-of-the-product-role/)
**Author**: Marty Cagan (featuring Guest: Benedict Evans) | **Date**: Aug 10, 2026

---

## Summary

Marty Cagan argues that the product role remains essential in the AI era because most people lack the distinctive skills to identify problems, discover solutions, and understand business viability—even with improved tools—and are not naturally tool builders.

## Key Takeaways

- **Recognize problem abstraction**: Develop the ability to see the general problem behind specific pain points, not just the surface-level symptoms, which requires deliberate practice and skill development.
- **Distinguish usage from creation**: Understand that being excellent at using a tool (e.g., sales) is fundamentally different from building tools (e.g., sales software), and this gap won't close with better AI tooling.
- **Map solutions across the business**: Build deep knowledge of how proposed solutions impact and integrate across your entire organization—sales, marketing, finance, compliance, legal—to ensure viability beyond just customer value.
- **Don't confuse customers with creators**: Avoid assuming customers think like product builders; most people and companies lack the mindset and skills to build tools themselves, making professional product teams more valuable, not less, in the AI era.
